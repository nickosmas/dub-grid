title: "Security and Sessions"
description: "Change your password, use two-factor authentication, manage where you're signed in, and reset a forgotten password."
---

Your security settings are under **Security** in your profile on the web, and **Profile > Security & sessions** in the DubGrid app.

## Your password

A password needs at least 10 characters, a letter and a number, and an uppercase letter or a symbol. Very long passwords are capped at 72 characters, fewer with accents or emoji.

To change it, enter your current password (and your authenticator code if you use two-factor), then the new password twice, and confirm. **Changing your password signs you out everywhere**, including the device you're using, so sign in again with the new password. The new password must differ from the current one.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor adds a second step to signing in: a 6-digit code from an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password. It's optional, but once it's on, every sign-in asks for a code.

- **On the web**: select **Enable 2FA**, confirm your password, scan the QR code (or enter the code shown), and enter the 6-digit code from your app to finish.
- **In the app**: add a new entry in your authenticator app with the key shown, then enter its 6-digit code.

If a code doesn't match, check that your phone's clock is correct and that you're using the newest DubGrid entry in your authenticator app. Turning two-factor off asks for a fresh code.

If you lose your authenticator, contact DubGrid support. After support resets two-factor for you, DubGrid asks you to **Set up two-factor again** the next time you sign in.

## Confirming it's you

Some changes ask you to **Confirm your identity** first, with your password, or your authenticator code if you use two-factor: changing your email, turning two-factor on or off, signing out other devices, deleting your account, and approving someone else's account deletion. A confirmation lasts five minutes.

## Where you're signed in

**On the web**, **Sessions** lists the browsers and devices signed in to your account, with **This device** marked, the browser or app version, the approximate location, and when each was last active. Older sessions are grouped under **Stale sessions**. Select **Sign out** on any one, or:

- **Sign out other devices** keeps you signed in here.
- **Sign out everywhere** includes this device.

**In the app**, **Devices** lists the same; tap a device to see where it signed in from and to sign it out.

After 30 minutes without activity on the web, DubGrid asks **Still there?** and signs you out a minute later unless you choose **Stay signed in**.

## New sign-in alerts

When your account signs in from a device DubGrid hasn't seen before, you get an email, "New sign-in to your DubGrid account", with the app, time, approximate location, and device. If it wasn't you, change your password, sign out the sessions you don't recognize, and contact DubGrid support. You're also emailed when two-factor is turned on or off, and when an administrator changes your sign-in email, both the old and the new address are told.

## App lock

In the app, **Require unlock on this device** asks for Face ID, your fingerprint, or your passcode whenever you return to DubGrid. It needs a passcode or biometrics set up on your phone first.

## Forgot your password

- **On the web**: select **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page, enter your email, and select **Send Reset Link**. The email's link lets you set a new password, after your authenticator code if you use two-factor. If the link has expired, request a new one.
- **In the app**: select **Forgot password?**, enter your email, and select **Send reset code**. Enter the 6-digit code from the email (you can resend it after a minute), then your authenticator code if you use two-factor, then your new password.

Resetting your password signs you out everywhere. Too many attempts in a row ask you to wait a few minutes before trying again.
