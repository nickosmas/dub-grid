## Notifications

**On the web**, **Alerts** in your profile lists each kind of alert with two switches: **In-App** and **Email**.

| Category                              | Default          |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| **Schedule Changes**                  | In-app           |
| **Shift Requests**                    | In-app           |
| **Membership & Invitations**          | In-app           |
| **Account & Organization**            | In-app           |
| **Billing & Payments** (Super Admins) | In-app and email |
| **Security Alerts**                   | Always on        |
| **System Notifications**              | In-app           |

Security alerts, such as a password or email change, a new device, or two-factor changes, can't be turned off. DubGrid sends at most 10 alert emails an hour.

**In the DubGrid app**, **Profile > Notifications** has **Push to this device**, for updates even when the app is closed, and in-app and email switches for **Schedule updates**, **Shift requests**, and **System & account**. If you turned notifications off for DubGrid on your phone, turn them on again in your phone's settings.

See [Alerts](/features/alerts) for what alerts look like and where they take you.

## Cookies and analytics

The first time you visit on the web, DubGrid asks about cookies: **Accept all**, **Essential only**, or **Customize**. Essential cookies are needed for signing in, security, and core features, including error monitoring, and can't be turned off. Analytics and performance cookies are optional. Change your choice any time under **Data & privacy > Manage cookie preferences** in your profile, or on the cookie policy page.

The first time you open the app, it asks the same with **Accept all** or **Essential only**. Change it later under **Profile > Privacy & data**, where **Analytics** shares anonymous usage data.

## Terms and policies

When the Terms of Service change, DubGrid asks you to read and accept them before you continue: scroll to the end on the web, or **Accept and continue** in the app. The Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Cookie Policy are linked from **Data & privacy** in your profile and **Privacy & data** in the app.

## Your data

The Privacy Policy explains what DubGrid keeps and for how long. To delete your account, see [Your profile](/account/profile). For other requests about your data, contact DubGrid support.
