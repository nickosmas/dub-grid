title: "Your Profile"
description: "Your contact details, work information, calendar subscription, and account, on the web and in the DubGrid app."
---

The profile holds your own details. On the web, open the menu with your name at the top of the page and choose **Profile**. In the DubGrid app, use the **Profile** tab.

## On the web

The profile looks like a person's page under People. The header shows your name, with a star for an Admin or Super Admin, then your roles, schedule areas and employment type, and your email and phone. If you're on the staff list and anything other than Active, the card under it says where your status stands.

It has two tabs:

- **Account**: opens first, with your details in place to edit. **Your details**, **Work**, **Access**, your record, **Sign-in and security** (see [Security and sessions](/account/security-and-sessions)), **Alerts** (see [Notifications, privacy, and data](/account/notification-privacy-and-data-controls)), **Data and privacy** (cookie preferences and policies), and **Delete account** last.
- **Schedule**, if you're on the schedule: your hours, shifts, and average hours per week over the last 12 weeks, with **What you work** under them; **This week**, your published shifts as on your dashboard; your **Recurring schedule** when you have one; your **Calendar subscription**; and your shifts and time off over the last 12 weeks as a month calendar, or as a list grouped by week with **Show as list**, with **Export CSV**.

### Your details

**Your details**, on **Account**, shows your name, email, and phone. Change your email or phone and select **Save changes** in the bar that appears (in the app, the checkmark at the top):

- Changing your email asks you to confirm your identity first. We then email a link to your current address and your new one, and your email changes once you confirm both.
- If someone else changed your record at the same time, DubGrid asks you to review the latest values and try again.

If you manage people, or you're a Super Admin, you edit your name and your work details here too. Otherwise, select **Request a name change**, enter the name you'd like and an optional note, and select **Send request**. The people who manage staff approve or reject it, and you can cancel it while it's pending.

**Access** shows your access role and the permissions it carries. **Administrative Teams** shows the teams you're on outside the schedule, if any.

### Your calendar

In **Schedule**, **Calendar subscription** adds your shifts to Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Outlook in one click, or gives you a link for any other calendar app. Select **Create private link**. Calendar apps check the link on their own, without signing in, so keep it private.

- **Replace link** makes a new link; the old one stops working immediately.
- **Disable subscription** stops calendar apps receiving updates until you create a new link.

## In the DubGrid app

The **Profile** tab shows your organization and departments, then **Profile details**, **Security & sessions**, **Notifications**, **Calendar subscription**, **Privacy & data**, and **Appearance**.

In **Profile details**, phone changes apply straight away. An email change asks you to confirm your identity, then changes once you confirm the links sent to your current and new addresses. Name changes go to your admins for review. If you manage people and take yourself off a schedule area where you have shifts from today on, you're asked whether to clear or keep those shifts first, as when [taking anyone off a schedule area](/staff/roster#taking-someone-off-a-schedule-area). **Calendar subscription** adds your shifts to your phone's calendar, Google, or Outlook, or shares the link.

## Deleting your account

- **Most people** ask for it: on the web, **Request account deletion** at the bottom of **Account**; in the app, **Security & sessions > Request account deletion**. A Super Admin approves it before the account is removed. You can cancel the request while it's pending.
- **Super Admins** delete their own account on the web: select **Delete account** at the bottom of **Account**, type `DELETE MY ACCOUNT`, and confirm your identity. It removes you from every organization you belong to. If you're an organization's only Super Admin, make someone else a Super Admin first.

Deleting an account can't be undone.

## Signing out

Signing out takes you to a goodbye page with **Sign back in**. It also says why, if DubGrid signed you out for you: after 30 minutes without activity on the web, or everywhere after your password changed.

## More than one organization

If you belong to several organizations, sign in at each organization's own address on the web. In the app, **Switch organization** on the **Profile** tab moves between them.
