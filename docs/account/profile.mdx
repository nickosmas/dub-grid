title: "Your Profile"

The profile holds your own details. On the web, open the menu with your name at the top of the page and choose **Profile**. In the DubGrid app, use the **Profile** tab.

## On the web

The profile looks like a person's page under People. The header shows your name, with a star for an Admin or Super Admin, then your roles, focus areas and employment type, your email and phone, and **Edit details**, which opens **Account** at your details. If you're on the staff list, the card under it says where your status stands.

It has three tabs:

- **Overview**: your hours, shifts, and average hours per week over the last 12 weeks; **This week**, your published shifts as on your dashboard; and **What you work**, your shifts and jobs as a chart, with a table view. Under **About you**: **Work** (focus areas, certification, roles, and internal notes), **Access** (your access level and the departments you manage, if any), and **Record** (your employee ID and when you joined). Someone who manages people but isn't on the schedule sees **About you** only.
- **Schedule**, if you're on the schedule: your **Recurring schedule** when you have one, your **Calendar subscription**, and your shifts and time off over the last 12 weeks as a month calendar, or as a list grouped by week with **Show as list**, with **Export CSV**.
- **Account**: **Your details**, **Sign-in and security** (see [Security and sessions](/account/security-and-sessions)), **Alerts** (see [Notifications, privacy, and data](/account/notification-privacy-and-data-controls)), and **Data and privacy**: cookie preferences, policies, and deleting your account.

### Your details

**Profile details**, under **Account > Your details**, shows your name, email, and phone. Change your email or phone and select **Save changes** (in the app, **Save** at the top):

- Changing your email asks you to confirm your identity first, then sends a confirmation to the new address.
- If someone else changed your record at the same time, DubGrid asks you to review the latest values and try again.

If you manage people, or you're a Super Admin, you edit your name and your work details here too. Otherwise, use **Name change requests**: enter the name you'd like and an optional note, and select **Request name change**. The people who manage staff approve or reject it, and you can cancel it while it's pending.

**Access** shows your role and the permissions it carries. **Management departments** shows the departments you manage, if any.

### Your calendar

In **Schedule**, **Calendar subscription** adds your shifts to Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Outlook in one click, or gives you a link for any other calendar app. Select **Create private link**. Calendar apps check the link on their own, without signing in, so keep it private.

- **Replace link** makes a new link; the old one stops working immediately.
- **Disable subscription** stops calendar apps receiving updates until you create a new link.

## In the DubGrid app

The **Profile** tab shows your organization and departments, then **Profile details**, **Security & sessions**, **Notifications**, **Calendar subscription**, **Privacy & data**, and **Appearance**.

In **Profile details**, email and phone changes apply straight away (an email change asks you to confirm your identity). Name changes go to your admins for review. If you manage people and take yourself off a focus area where you have shifts from today on, you're asked whether to clear or keep those shifts first, as when [taking anyone off a focus area](/staff/roster#taking-someone-off-a-focus-area). **Calendar subscription** adds your shifts to your phone's calendar, Google, or Outlook, or shares the link.

## Deleting your account

- **Most people** ask for it: on the web, **Request account deletion** in your profile; in the app, **Security & sessions > Request account deletion**. The people who manage staff approve it before the account is removed. You can cancel the request while it's pending.
- **Super Admins** delete their own account from their profile on the web: select **Delete account**, type `DELETE MY ACCOUNT`, and confirm your identity. It removes you from every organization you belong to. If you're an organization's only Super Admin, make someone else a Super Admin first.

Deleting an account can't be undone.

## Signing out

Signing out takes you to a goodbye page with **Sign back in**. It also says why, if DubGrid signed you out for you: after 30 minutes without activity on the web, or everywhere after your password changed.

## More than one organization

If you belong to several organizations, sign in at each organization's own address on the web. In the app, **Switch organization** on the **Profile** tab moves between them.
