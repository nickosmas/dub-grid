Your profile holds your own details. On the web, open the menu with your name at the top of the page and choose **Profile**. In the DubGrid app, use the **Profile** tab.

## On the web

The profile has these sections:

- **Profile**: your details, access, and requests.
- **Security**: your password, two-factor, and sessions. See [Security and sessions](/account/security-and-sessions).
- **Alerts**: how DubGrid contacts you. See [Notifications, privacy, and data](/account/notification-privacy-and-data-controls).
- **Overview**: your role, focus areas, certifications, recurring schedule, and calendar subscription, if you're on the schedule.
- **Data & privacy**: cookie preferences and policies.

### Your details

**Profile details** shows your name, email, and phone. Change your email or phone and select **Save changes**:

- Changing your email asks you to confirm your identity first, then sends a confirmation to the new address.
- If someone else changed your record at the same time, DubGrid asks you to review the latest values and try again.

If you manage people, or you're a Super Admin, you edit your name and your work details here too. Otherwise, use **Name change requests**: enter the name you'd like and an optional note, and select **Request name change**. The people who manage staff approve or reject it, and you can cancel it while it's pending.

**Access** shows your role and the permissions it carries. **Management departments** shows the departments you manage, if any.

### Your calendar

In **Overview**, **Calendar subscription** adds your shifts to Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Outlook in one click, or gives you a link for any other calendar app. Select **Create private link**. Calendar apps check the link on their own, without signing in, so keep it private.

- **Replace link** makes a new link; the old one stops working immediately.
- **Disable subscription** stops calendar apps receiving updates until you create a new link.

## In the DubGrid app

The **Profile** tab shows your organization and departments, then **Profile details**, **Security & sessions**, **Notifications**, **Calendar subscription**, **Privacy & data**, and **Appearance**.

In **Profile details**, email and phone changes apply straight away (an email change asks you to confirm your identity). Name changes go to your admins for review. **Calendar subscription** adds your shifts to your phone's calendar, Google, or Outlook, or shares the link.

## Deleting your account

- **Most people** ask for it: on the web, **Request account deletion** in your profile; in the app, **Security & sessions > Request account deletion**. The people who manage staff approve it before the account is removed. You can cancel the request while it's pending.
- **Super Admins** delete their own account from their profile on the web: select **Delete account**, type `DELETE MY ACCOUNT`, and confirm your identity. It removes you from every organization you belong to. If you're an organization's only Super Admin, make someone else a Super Admin first.

Deleting an account can't be undone.

## Signing out

Signing out takes you to a goodbye page with **Sign back in**. It also says why, if DubGrid signed you out for you: after 30 minutes without activity on the web, or everywhere after your password changed.

## More than one organization

If you belong to several organizations, sign in at each organization's own address on the web. In the app, **Switch organization** on the **Profile** tab moves between them.
