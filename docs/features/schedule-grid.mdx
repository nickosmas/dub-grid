The **Schedule** tab shows your organization's schedule as a grid: people down the side, days across the top. Everyone sees the published schedule. People allowed to edit shifts also see and change drafts; see [Draft and publish](/features/draft-publish).

## Reading the grid

- **Rows** are grouped by department and, inside each department, by schedule area. Schedule areas without a department sit under **Ungrouped**. Your own row is tinted and today's column is marked.
- **Each section** ends with an **Open Shifts** row showing shifts that still need someone, and count rows for each kind of shift showing how many are scheduled against how many are needed.
- **A cell** shows the person's shifts or absence. A person can hold more than one shift in a day. An absence is drawn in diagonal stripes of its own color, so it never reads as a shift. Items sit in time order and items that run straight into each other, such as two shifts that meet or a shift and the absence that covers the rest of it, are drawn joined; a gap keeps them apart. An absence beside a shift shows the time it covers, with the next day named on a time past midnight, and comes first when the person arrived late.
- **Shift requests** waiting on a day show as a small fold in the top-left corner of the shift they concern: gray while a teammate has to answer, amber while a manager has to decide. See [Requests on the schedule](/features/shift-requests#requests-on-the-schedule).
- **Schedule notes** appear as coloured dots in the bottom-right corner of their shift: up to two, then a count such as **+2** for more. A hollow dot is a note not yet published. Hover over the dots to read every note. The month view, the phone-sized view, the dashboard and the printout mark notes the same way, and the printout's legend shows each note's colour beside its name.

If a period hasn't been published yet, people who can't edit the schedule see **This period has not been published yet** instead of drafts. They can select only their own shifts; every other cell is there to read and doesn't respond to a click. The schedule follows your Organization's time zone, so **Today** and the active date stay consistent for everyone.

## Choosing what you see

Use the toolbar above the grid:

| Control                            | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1 Week**, **2 Weeks**, **Month** | How much of the schedule to show. Two weeks is the default; on a narrower screen the grid shows one week at a time.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Arrows and **Today**               | Move back or forward a whole period, or jump to the current one. Two-week periods line up with your organization's pay period.                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Schedule area tabs                 | Show **All** or one schedule area. The tabs appear when your organization has more than one. Up to **All** and four areas show at once, fewer when the names are long; scroll the tabs for the rest.                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Sort staff**                     | Order people by **Seniority** or **Alphabetical**. On a window narrower than 1700 pixels it's an arrows button.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Search                             | Highlight the people whose names match. On a window narrower than 1700 pixels it's a magnifier button: the field opens in its place, and the buttons to its right show only their icons until it closes. **Esc** clears what you typed and then closes it, and the button stays highlighted while a search is active. On a phone the field is always shown. |
| **Schedule Builder**               | Auto-build the draft and compare the schedule with your coverage requirements, day by day, if you can see dashboard analytics.                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Shift requests**                 | Open the [shift request board](/features/shift-requests). Its count is the requests on your **To approve** tab if you can approve requests, otherwise the requests asking for your answer. It shows 0 when there are none.                                                                                                                                  |

On a window narrower than 1440 pixels, **Schedule Builder**, **Shift requests** and **More tools** show only their icons, so the toolbar stays on one row; hover one to see its name. **Shift requests** keeps its count.

Weeks start on Sunday. The **Month** view is for reading. Each day lists its focus areas with how many people work each shift, how many are absent, and marks for open shifts, changes not yet published and requests waiting for an answer. If you can see dashboard analytics, a day from today on that is short of your coverage requirements says how many people it needs. Select a day to see everyone working with their shift times, the open shifts, and who is absent or has a day off; **Open this week** takes you to that week to make changes. The arrow keys move between days.

On a phone-sized screen the month is a small calendar with the selected day listed under it, and the week views show one day at a time.

## Editing shifts

You need permission to edit shifts. Every change you make is a draft until it's published.

- **Add a shift**: select an empty cell. The panel lists your organization's shifts grouped by shift, each with its times, then the absences. Tiles always spell out each shift and absence in full, whatever the grid shows.
- **Preferences**: when the person has [shift or job preferences](/features/recurring-shifts#preferences), a note at the top of the list names them, such as "Shift preference: Prefers Day Shift", and the shift they name is marked **Preferred** or **Only**. Any shift can still be chosen. When a shift goes outside an Only preference, the panel says so under the shift, for example "Day Shift is outside Aisha's preference. Shift preference: Only Evening Shift" or "Med Aide job is outside Aisha's preference. Job preference: Only CNA", and the shift saves as usual. Absences and work with no set shift never get this note.
- **Change a shift**: select a filled cell and it opens ready to edit. Adjust the times if this shift is different, or select **Reset to standard shift time** to put them back. When you change a shift that was already scheduled, the panel shows it before and after; adding to an empty day shows no comparison. Select **Save**, or **Discard** to put it back as it was. Closing the panel or selecting another cell with changes you haven't saved asks whether to save them, discard them, or keep editing. A repeating shift opens on its details first; see [Recurring shifts](/features/recurring-shifts).
- **Two shifts in a day**: select **Add another shift**. The panel shows the shift already worked. When your organization prevents conflicts, shifts that overlap it are listed under **Can't be added** with the reason.
- **Remove a shift**: in the panel, select **Remove** on the shift or absence you want to take off, or the button below them to clear the cell. It names what goes, such as **Remove shift**, **Remove both shifts**, **Remove shift and absence** or **Remove absence**. Either way you confirm first, and clearing the cell is saved to the draft as soon as you do.
- **Absence beside a shift**: when someone misses part of a shift, select **Add an absence** in the panel and pick the absence, such as Day then Sick. Choose **Left early** and set **Left at**, or **Arrived late** and set **Arrives at**: the absence covers the rest of the shift, or its start, and carries no hours. A cell holds up to two shifts and one absence. Leaving during the first of two shifts takes the second off, and the absence covers it too. A time that leaves the absence nothing to cover can't be saved, and leaving as the shift starts offers to make it an absence for the whole day. A bar under the cards shows the time worked and the absence, and removing the absence puts the shifts back. A shift with an absence can't be made repeating.
- **Away with a return date**: someone [away until a date](/staff/roster#away-with-dates) stays on the grid. From the day they were marked away until the return date, their days show as one **Away** bar per week, naming the absence those days hold if one was picked, such as **Away · Vacation**. They can't be scheduled, and hovering a day says why. Their days before they went away show what they worked and can't be changed while they're away; from the return date their days work as usual. Marking someone away changes their days in the draft (their shifts come off, or the days hold the absence), so until the period is published the bar has a dashed outline and a chip such as **Removed ×3**; hover it to see each day's Before and After. With **Highlight changes** on, the bar shows the published change the same way.
- **Away all period**: someone away, or on a whole-day absence other than the day off absence, on every day shown moves to the end of their section under an **Away all period** row with how many there are. Select the row to fold them away; folded, it names each person and why, such as **Away until Nov 9** or **Vacation every day**, and says how many unpublished changes they hold. Your browser remembers the fold. When everyone in a section is away, there is no group.
- **Away planned for later**: someone with [time away that starts later](/staff/roster#away-with-dates) shows the same **Away** bar from the start date to the day before they're back, and works as usual before it.
- **Last day**: someone with a [last day](/staff/roster#a-last-day) works as usual up to and on it. The days after it show as one **Offboarded** bar per week and can't be scheduled; hovering a day names the last day.
- **First day**: someone with a [first day](/staff/roster#a-first-day) still ahead shows one **Starts** bar per week, such as **Starts Nov 10**, on the days before it. Those days can't be scheduled, and hovering one names the first day.
- **Suspended**: someone [suspended](/staff/roster#active-away-suspended-and-offboarded) keeps their row and can still be scheduled. A lock beside their name says they won't see it in the app, and **Assign open shift** says the same under their name.
- **Past days**: days before today have a light shade, and nothing moves into or out of them: they can't be dragged, pasted into, cleared from the right-click menu, selected for **Bulk Delete Entries**, or filled from recurring schedules or by **Auto-build**. Select a past cell to **Correct this day** if you can both edit shifts and publish: change the shift or absence, type a reason if you like, check **Before** and **After**, and select **Save correction**. The published day changes right away, without a draft, and staff aren't sent an alert. A corrected day carries a small history mark (a clock turning back) in the top-right corner of the cell. Hovering it shows a **Corrected day** label with what the day was before it was corrected, when it was corrected, by whom, and the reason; opening the day shows the same above the shift, without restating a shift the correction added. An Admin can correct the last 14 days; an earlier day needs a Super Admin. Anyone else sees a past day as it is.
- **Right-click a cell** for **Copy**, **Paste**, and **Clear day**. Pasting over an existing entry asks before replacing it. <kbd>Cmd</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> with <kbd>C</kbd> and <kbd>V</kbd> copy and paste too.
- **Drag and drop**: drag a shift to another cell to move it, or hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> while dragging to copy it. The shift you drop replaces what was in that cell. DubGrid checks that the person can work that shift first.
- **Schedule notes**: turn each note on or off with its switch in the panel's **Schedule notes** section, if you can edit notes. On a day with two shifts, each shift has its own. A note belongs to the shift you selected, so it stays with that shift when the schedule changes.
- **Repeating shifts**: set a shift to repeat from the panel's **Repeating** section. See [Recurring shifts](/features/recurring-shifts).

The panel's **Created by** and **Updated by** lines show who last touched a shift.

## Filling open shifts

If you can edit shifts, select a shift in an **Open Shifts** row to see the people who can take it: those with the right schedule area, role, and certification, and no absence or overlapping shift that day. Choose someone to put them on it as a draft.

People who can't edit the schedule can offer to take an open shift instead; their manager approves it. See [Shift requests](/features/shift-requests).

See [Coverage and Open Shifts](/features/coverage-and-open-shifts) for the difference between a coverage gap and an open shift, who can see each one, and how call-off vacancies work.

## More tools

The **More tools** menu holds the rest, depending on what you're allowed to do. If you can't edit shifts, the menu would hold only **Print**, so **Print** is a button on the toolbar instead.

- **Print**: choose the view, font size, and schedule areas, then **Preview & Print**. With full shift names, the printout covers one week and needs no shift legend. The printout is laid out like the grid and includes a legend for your shift and absence codes and schedule notes. Printing may not be available in every organization.
- **Export CSV** and **Publish History**, for people who edit shifts.
- **Fill from Recurring Schedules**: fill empty cells from recurring schedules.
- **Import Previous Schedule**: copy the previous period into this one as drafts.
- **Bulk Delete Entries**: select many entries at once and review them before removing them. Available on a desktop-sized screen in the week views.
- **Authors**: show who made each draft change.

For the full workflow for importing a period, reviewing a batch deletion, viewing publication history, printing, and exporting, see [Schedule tools](/features/schedule-tools).

## On the DubGrid app

The app's **Schedule** tab shows the team's schedule, and selecting a shift opens its details. From your own shift you can **Drop shift** (offer it for pickup or call off) or **Swap**. Building and publishing the schedule happen on the web.
