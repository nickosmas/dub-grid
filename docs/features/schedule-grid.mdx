The schedule grid is where you will spend most of your time in DubGrid. Each row represents a staff member, and each column represents a date. Cells show the shift code assigned to that employee on that day.

## View modes

The grid supports three view modes. Use the view toggle in the toolbar to switch between them.

| Mode      | Description                                                             |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1W**    | One-week view, seven columns, Sunday through Saturday                   |
| **2W**    | Two-week view (default), fourteen columns across two consecutive weeks  |
| **Month** | Calendar month view, shows the full calendar grid for the current month |

## Navigating dates

Use the date navigator buttons in the toolbar to move through time:

- **Back** - moves backward by one full view span (one week, two weeks, or one month)
- **Today** - jumps directly to the period containing today's date
- **Forward** - moves forward by one full view span

Today's date column is always highlighted so you can orient yourself at a glance.

## Editing a shift

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click a cell">
    Click any cell in the grid to open the **Shift Edit Panel** on the right side of the screen. The
    panel shows the employee name and date for context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a shift code">
    Choose a shift code from the list of your organization's configured codes. You can also select
    multiple codes for split or multi-part shifts. To clear a shift, select **OFF** or remove all
    codes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set custom times (optional)">
    Adjust the start and end times for the shift if the defaults don't apply. Custom times override
    the shift code's default schedule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Changes save automatically as drafts. The cell updates immediately and shows a visual draft
    indicator.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  All edits are saved as drafts and are not visible to staff until you publish them. See [Draft &
  Publish](/features/draft-publish) for details.
</Tip>

## Drag and drop

You can move a shift from one employee or date to another by dragging:

1. Click and hold a shift cell until it lifts.
2. Drag it to the target cell, a different employee row, a different date column, or both.
3. Release to drop. The move is saved as a draft immediately.

If the target cell already has a shift, the move replaces it (as a draft).

## Focus area filter

The toolbar includes a **Focus Area** filter that lets you narrow the grid to a specific section of your facility. Select **All** to see every staff member, or choose any configured focus area (wing, department, or section) to show only the employees assigned there.

Your organization may use a custom label for focus areas, the filter label in the toolbar reflects that terminology.

## Staff search

While a focus area is active, use the **Search** field in the toolbar to filter the visible staff rows by name. The grid highlights matching employees as you type.

## Printing

DubGrid generates a print-optimized layout for your schedule.

1. Click the **Print** button in the toolbar.
2. In the **Print Options** dialog, select which focus areas to include.
3. Choose the date range to print.
4. Configure additional options such as orientation and whether to include the shift code legend.
5. Click **Print** to send to your printer or save as a PDF.

The printed output includes your organization name, the date the schedule was printed, and the selected date range. Shift codes are shown with their colors, and a legend appears at the bottom.
