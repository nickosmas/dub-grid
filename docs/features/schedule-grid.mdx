The **Schedule** tab shows your organization's schedule as a grid: people down the side, days across the top. Everyone sees the published schedule. People allowed to edit shifts also see and change drafts; see [Draft and publish](/features/draft-publish).

## Reading the grid

- **Rows** are grouped by department and, inside each department, by focus area. Focus areas without a department sit under **Ungrouped**. Your own row is tinted and today's column is marked.
- **Each section** ends with an **Open Shifts** row showing shifts that still need someone, and count rows for each kind of shift showing how many are scheduled against how many are needed.
- **A cell** shows the person's shifts or absence. A person can hold more than one shift in a day. An absence is drawn in diagonal stripes of its own color, so it never reads as a shift. Items sit in time order and items that run straight into each other, such as two shifts that meet or a shift and the absence that covers the rest of it, are drawn joined; a gap keeps them apart. An absence beside a shift shows the time it covers, with the next day named on a time past midnight, and comes first when the person arrived late.
- **Schedule notes** appear as coloured dots along the bottom of their shift: up to two, then a count such as **+2** for more. A hollow dot is a note not yet published. Hover over the dots to read every note.

If a period hasn't been published yet, people who can't edit the schedule see **This period has not been published yet** instead of drafts. The schedule follows your Organization's time zone, so **Today** and the active date stay consistent for everyone.

## Choosing what you see

Use the toolbar above the grid:

| Control                            | What it does                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **1 Week**, **2 Weeks**, **Month** | How much of the schedule to show. Two weeks is the default; on a narrower screen the grid shows one week at a time.            |
| Arrows and **Today**               | Move back or forward a whole period, or jump to the current one. Two-week periods line up with your organization's pay period. |
| Focus area tabs                    | Show **All** or one focus area. The tabs appear when your organization has more than one.                                      |
| **Sort Staff**                     | Order people by **Seniority** or **Alphabetical**.                                                                             |
| Search                             | Highlight the people whose names match.                                                                                        |
| **Schedule Builder**               | Auto-build the draft and compare the schedule with your coverage requirements, day by day, if you can see dashboard analytics. |

Weeks start on Sunday. The **Month** view is for reading; switch to a week view to make changes. On a phone-sized screen the web app shows one day at a time.

## Editing shifts

You need permission to edit shifts. Every change you make is a draft until it's published.

- **Add a shift**: select an empty cell. The panel lists your organization's shifts grouped by shift, each with its times, then the absences. Tiles always spell out each shift and absence in full, whatever the grid shows.
- **Change a shift**: select a filled cell and it opens ready to edit. Adjust the times if this shift is different, or select **Reset to standard shift time** to put them back. When you change a shift that was there, the panel shows it before and after; adding to an empty day shows no comparison. Select **Save changes**, or **Cancel** to leave it as it was. A repeating shift opens on its details first; see [Recurring shifts](/features/recurring-shifts).
- **Two shifts in a day**: select **Add another shift**. The panel shows the shift already worked. When your organization prevents conflicts, shifts that overlap it are listed under **Can't be added** with the reason.
- **Remove a shift**: in the panel, select **Remove** on the shift or absence you want to take off, or the button below them to clear the cell. It names what goes, such as **Remove shift**, **Remove both shifts**, **Remove shift and absence** or **Remove absence**. Either way you confirm first, and clearing the cell is saved to the draft as soon as you do.
- **Absence beside a shift**: when someone misses part of a shift, select **Add an absence** in the panel and pick the absence, such as Day then Sick. Choose **Left early** and set **Left at**, or **Arrived late** and set **Arrives at**: the absence covers the rest of the shift, or its start, and carries no hours. A cell holds up to two shifts and one absence. Leaving during the first of two shifts takes the second off, and the absence covers it too. A time that leaves the absence nothing to cover can't be saved, and leaving as the shift starts offers to make it an absence for the whole day. A bar under the cards shows the time worked and the absence, and removing the absence puts the shifts back. A shift with an absence can't be made repeating.
- **Away with a return date**: someone [away until a date](/staff/roster#away-until-a-date) stays on the grid. Their days before the return date show as one **Away** bar per week and can't be scheduled; from the return date their days work as usual.
- **Right-click a cell** for **Copy Entry**, **Paste Entry**, and **Remove Entry**. Pasting over an existing entry asks before replacing it. <kbd>Cmd</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> with <kbd>C</kbd> and <kbd>V</kbd> copy and paste too.
- **Drag and drop**: drag a shift to another cell to move it, or hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> while dragging to copy it. The shift you drop replaces what was there. DubGrid checks that the person can work that shift first.
- **Schedule notes**: turn each note on or off with its switch in the panel's **Schedule notes** section, if you can edit notes. On a day with two shifts, each shift has its own. A note belongs to the shift you selected, so it stays with that shift when the schedule changes.
- **Repeating shifts**: set a shift to repeat from the panel's **Repeating** section. See [Recurring shifts](/features/recurring-shifts).

The panel's **Created by** and **Updated by** lines show who last touched a shift.

## Filling open shifts

If you can edit shifts, select a shift in an **Open Shifts** row to see the people who can take it: those with the right focus area, role, and certification, and no absence or overlapping shift that day. Choose someone to put them on it as a draft.

People who can't edit the schedule can offer to take an open shift instead; their manager approves it. See [Shift requests](/features/shift-requests).

See [Coverage and Open Shifts](/features/coverage-and-open-shifts) for the difference between a coverage gap and an open shift, who can see each one, and how call-off vacancies work.

## More tools

The **Tools** menu holds the rest, depending on what you're allowed to do:

- **Requests**: the shift request board.
- **Print**: choose the view, font size, and focus areas, then **Preview & Print**. With full shift names, the printout covers one week and needs no shift legend. The printout includes a legend for your shift codes and schedule notes. Printing may not be available in every organization.
- **Export CSV** and **Publish History**, for people who edit shifts.
- **Fill from Recurring Schedules**: fill empty cells from recurring schedules.
- **Import Previous Schedule**: copy the previous period into this one as drafts.
- **Bulk Delete Entries**: select many entries at once and review them before removing them. Available on a desktop-sized screen in the week views.
- **Authors**: show who made each draft change.

For the full workflow for importing a period, reviewing a batch deletion, viewing publication history, printing, and exporting, see [Schedule tools](/features/schedule-tools).

## On the DubGrid app

The app's **Schedule** tab shows the team's schedule, and selecting a shift opens its details. From your own shift you can **Drop shift** (offer it for pickup or call off) or **Swap**. Building and publishing the schedule happen on the web.
