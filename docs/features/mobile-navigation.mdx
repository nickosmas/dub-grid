The DubGrid mobile app puts the schedule, requests, people, alerts, and your account on your phone. What you see depends on your role, just like on the web.

## First launch

The first time you open the app, a few short screens introduce it: **Your schedule, always with you**, **Cover shifts on the go**, and **Know when things change**. The last one offers to **Enable notifications** for schedule updates, shift requests, and account and sign-in alerts; choose **Not now** to decide later in your profile. **Skip** jumps straight to signing in.

## Signing in

1. Enter your organization's subdomain (the `yourorg` part of `yourorg.dubgrid.com`) and select **Continue**. **Need help with your subdomain?** explains where to find it.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password** and select **Sign in**. **Switch organization** goes back a step.
3. If you use an authenticator app, enter its 6-digit code and select **Verify and sign in**.

Invitations are accepted on the web first; see [Joining your team](/onboarding).

**Forgot password?** sends a 6-digit code to your email. Enter the code, then your authenticator code if you use one, then choose a new password. See [Security and sessions](/account/security-and-sessions).

## Before you start

The app may ask you to:

- choose your privacy settings (**Accept all** or **Essential only**), which you can change later under **Profile > Privacy & data**
- accept updated terms (**Accept and continue**, or **Sign out**)
- set up two-factor again, if DubGrid support has reset it for you

## The tabs

| Tab          | What's there                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Who sees it                                                               |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Home**     | Admins and Super Admins get a dashboard: coverage, your schedule, pending approvals, open shifts, overtime watch, recent activity, and unpublished changes. Everyone else gets **Your Week**, open shifts, and requests that need your response. | Everyone except a User who manages a department but isn't on the schedule |
| **Schedule** | The team schedule a week or a month at a time, filtered by focus area                                                                                                                                                                            | Everyone who can view the schedule                                        |
| **Requests** | **Available**, **Mine**, and **History**, plus **All** and **Approval** for people who handle requests                                                                                                                                           | People on the schedule, and people who can approve requests               |
| **People**   | The directory, with **Schedule** and **Management** views                                                                                                                                                                                        | Everyone                                                                  |
| **Profile**  | Your organization, your departments, and settings for your profile, security, notifications, calendar, privacy, and appearance                                                                                                                   | Everyone                                                                  |

Selecting a shift opens its details, where you can **Drop shift** (offer it for pickup or call off) or **Swap**. Selecting a person opens their page. The bell on the Schedule and Home headers opens your [alerts](/features/alerts). See [Schedule on the DubGrid App](/features/mobile-schedule) for schedule navigation, shift details, open shifts, and recovery states.

## App lock

Under **Profile > Security & sessions**, turn on **Require unlock on this device** to ask for Face ID, your fingerprint, or your device passcode whenever you come back to DubGrid. It needs a passcode or biometrics set up on your phone. When locked, the app shows **DubGrid is locked**; **Try again** or **Sign out**.

## When something goes wrong

- **No connection**: a **Network connection issue** banner asks you to check your connection. If the app couldn't load at all, a full screen offers **Try again**.
- **A tab fails**: it says **This tab ran into a problem**; **Try again** usually clears it.
- **Organization unavailable**: your organization is on hold, usually over billing. Super Admins see what's needed and can open billing on the web; everyone else can **Try again** later.

## Switching organization and signing out

If you belong to more than one organization, **Profile > Switch organization** moves between them. **Sign out** signs you out on this device. To sign out other devices too, use **Profile > Security & sessions**.

## What stays on the web

Building and publishing the schedule, reports, billing, Admin permissions, and organization settings are on the web only. When an alert is about one of these, the app says **Open this on the web to see more.**
