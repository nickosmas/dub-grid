The DubGrid mobile app puts the schedule, requests, people, alerts, and your account on your phone. What you see depends on your role, just like on the web.

## First launch

The first time you open the app, a few short screens introduce it: **Welcome to DubGrid**, **Your schedule, always with you**, **Cover shifts on the go**, and **Know when things change**. The last one offers to **Enable notifications** for schedule updates, shift requests, and account and sign-in alerts; choose **Not now** to decide later in your profile. **Skip** jumps to that notifications screen, or straight to signing in when your phone has already answered the notification question.

## Signing in

1. Enter your organization's domain (the `yourorg` part of `yourorg.dubgrid.com`) and select **Continue**. **Need help with your domain?** explains where to find it.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password** and select **Sign in**. **Switch organization** goes back a step.
3. If you use an authenticator app, enter its 6-digit code and select **Verify and sign in**.

Invitations are accepted on the web first; see [Joining your team](/onboarding).

**Forgot password?** sends a 6-digit code to your email. Enter the code, then your authenticator code if you use one, then choose a new password. See [Security and sessions](/account/security-and-sessions).

## Before you start

The app may ask you to:

- choose your privacy settings (**Accept all** or **Essential only**), which you can change later under **Profile > Privacy & data**
- accept updated terms (**Accept and continue**, or **Sign out**)
- set up two-factor again, if DubGrid support has reset it for you

## The tabs

| Tab          | What it shows                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Who sees it                                                                |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Home**     | Admins and Super Admins who work shifts switch between **My schedule** (next shift, requests that need your response, every open shift with **Claim** on the ones they can work, **Your week**) and **Team** (coverage, pending approvals, overtime watch, and recent activity); others see **Team** alone. Everyone else gets **Your week**, open shifts, and requests that need your response. | Everyone except a User on an administrative team who isn't on the schedule |
| **Schedule** | The team schedule a week or a month at a time, filtered by schedule area                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Everyone who can view the schedule                                         |
| **Requests** | **Available**, **Mine**, and **History**, plus **All** and **Approval** for people who handle requests                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | People on the schedule, and people who can approve requests                |
| **People**   | The directory, with **On Schedule** and **Administrative Teams** views                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Everyone                                                                   |
| **Profile**  | Your organization, your departments, and settings for your profile, security, notifications, calendar, privacy, and appearance                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Everyone                                                                   |

Selecting a shift opens its details, where you can **Drop shift** (offer it for pickup or call off) or **Swap**. Selecting a person opens their page. The bell on the Schedule and Home headers opens your [alerts](/features/alerts). See [Schedule on the DubGrid App](/features/mobile-schedule) for schedule navigation, shift details, open shifts, and recovery states.

## On a foldable iPhone

Opened or partly folded, the iPhone Duo shows each tab as two panes: the tab's own page on the left and, on the right, what you pick in it. Until you pick something, the right pane already shows something useful:

- **Home**: the month fills the left pane, as in Calendar, with arrows and **Today**, and Home sits beside it for the chosen day's week: **My schedule** or **Team**, as on the phone. A shift or day you tap opens over Home, with **Home** above it to go back.
- **Schedule**: the month fills the left pane, as in Calendar, with arrows and **Today**, and the right pane is the chosen day: coverage by schedule area for Admins and Super Admins, the day's open shifts and its requests, and for everyone else, first, your shift that day if you work it; then who works each shift, by schedule area. Tap a person to see their shift, and the day's name above it to go back.
- **Requests**: what approving or accepting the request does, when it was asked, and who works each day it touches, with its people and their shifts marked and listed first. Its card, with the buttons to answer it, stays in the list.
- **People**: the person's page, starting with the first person in the list.
- **Alerts**: what the alert points to, such as a shift, a person or a day, starting with the newest alert.
- **Profile**: the setting you choose, starting with **Profile details**.

On the iPhone Duo, open or closed, Home's and Schedule's alerts bell stands in the side column with the tabs. Partly folded, each pane takes its half of the screen. Folding the phone closed keeps a shift, person or setting you picked open as its own page, with Back; a request you picked stays highlighted in the list, and anything else returns to the list.

## App lock

Under **Profile > Security & sessions**, turn on **Require unlock on this device** to ask for Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode on iPhone, or your fingerprint, face, or screen lock on Android, whenever you come back to DubGrid. It needs a passcode or biometrics set up on your phone. When locked, the app shows **The app is locked**; **Unlock** or **Sign out**.

With app lock on, **Confirm your identity** uses Face ID, Touch ID, or your fingerprint instead of your password, after you've typed it once on that phone; see [Confirming it's you](/account/security-and-sessions#confirming-its-you).

## When something goes wrong

- **No connection**: when the connection has been gone for a few seconds, a **Network connection issue** banner asks you to check it. A request that drops is tried again on its own first. If the app couldn't load at all, a full screen offers **Try again**.
- **A tab fails**: it says **This tab ran into a problem**; **Try again** usually clears it.
- **Organization unavailable**: your organization is on hold, usually over billing. Super Admins see what's needed and, when billing is the cause, are told to manage billing on the web; everyone else can **Try again** later.

## Switching organization and signing out

If you belong to more than one organization, **Profile > Switch organization** moves between them. **Sign out** signs you out on this device. To sign out other devices too, use **Profile > Security & sessions**.

## What stays on the web

Building and publishing the schedule, reports, billing, Admin permissions, and organization settings are on the web only. When an alert is about one of these, the app says **Open this on the web to see more.**
