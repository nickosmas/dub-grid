---
title: "Schedule on the DubGrid App"
description: "Read the team schedule on your phone, open a shift, and handle the schedule actions available in the app."
---

The **Schedule** tab in the DubGrid app is for reading the published team schedule and acting on your own shifts. Building a schedule, editing shifts, recurring schedules, and publishing stay on the web.

## Find a day or week

Use the week strip to move to the previous or next week, or select **Today** to return to the current date. Open the month calendar to jump to another day. If your Organization has more than one focus area, choose one to narrow the team schedule.

The app shows the published schedule. Your own shifts remain easy to find, and selecting any shift opens its details. A shift can include more than one segment, so the details show each part in time order.

## Open a shift

Shift details show the date, time, focus area, job, absence, and schedule-note information that applies to that shift. On your own shift, you can:

- select **Drop shift**, then offer it for pickup or call off
- select **Swap** and choose an eligible teammate and date
- review or cancel a request that is still open or awaiting approval

An offer, swap, or call-off follows the same request lifecycle as the web. It does not change the schedule until the request is settled. See [Shift requests](/features/shift-requests).

## Open shifts and request feedback

When the app shows an open shift you can work, select it to offer coverage. The app confirms the action before it sends the request, then refreshes the schedule and requests list with the result. Managers can approve eligible requests in the **Requests** tab when their access allows it.

## When the schedule is unavailable

The app keeps the schedule screen readable while it loads, and you can pull to refresh after a temporary problem. It explains when the schedule cannot load, when the team schedule is unavailable, or when your account is not linked to a staff record. Select **Try again** after you reconnect or your access changes.

For all other recovery paths, including a full offline start, see [The DubGrid App](/features/mobile-navigation#when-something-goes-wrong).

## What stays on the web

Use the web app to build or publish schedules, change recurring patterns, import a previous period, fill coverage in bulk, and manage schedule settings. See [Schedule Grid](/features/schedule-grid) and [Schedule Tools](/features/schedule-tools).
