---
title: "Reports"
description: "Run reports on hours, activity, coverage, requests, and your roster, and export them."
---

The **Reports** tab turns your schedule and roster into tables you can filter and export. Super Admins can run reports, as can Admins with permission to view reports, which new Admins have by default. Reports are on the web only, and may not be available in every organization.

## Running a report

1. Choose a **Report**.
2. Choose a **Range**: **Current week**, **Pay period**, or **Custom** dates. Pay period needs your organization's pay period set in [Schedule Rules](/configuration/schedule-rules-and-display). Reports about your roster don't use a range.
3. Narrow it with the filters the report offers: **Focus areas**, **People**, **Shift categories**, **Jobs**, **Schedule notes**, or **Dates**.
4. Select **Generate report**. **Refresh** runs it again with the latest data.

## The reports

**Employee directory**, **Staff hours**, **Staff activity**, **Mentoring hours**, **Mentoring detail**, **Coverage**, **Period summary**, **Shift requests**, **Absences and call-offs**, **Roster status**, **Certifications and roles**, **Account access**, **Schedule matrix**, and **Schedule notes**.

- **Staff hours** shows each person's scheduled hours, shifts and days worked, breakdowns by shift and by job, schedule notes, absence days, and overtime hours.
- **Staff activity** adds off days, unscheduled days, and each person's shift request activity.

When nothing is published in the range, **Staff hours** says **No published schedule for this range.**

## Exporting

**Export PDF** or **Export CSV** saves the report as it's shown. Export is available once the report has rows.
