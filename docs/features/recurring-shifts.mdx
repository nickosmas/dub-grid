---
title: "Recurring Shifts"
description: "Define repeating shift patterns for employees and apply them to any date range in seconds."
---

Recurring shifts let you build schedule templates so you don't have to enter the same shift codes week after week. DubGrid supports two types of recurring shifts: day-of-week templates and shift series.

## Day-of-week templates

A day-of-week template assigns a shift code to a specific day of the week for a given employee. For example, you can set an employee to work a Day shift every Monday.

Each template includes:

- **Employee** - the staff member the template applies to
- **Shift code** - the shift assigned on that day
- **Day of week** - the day the template repeats on (Sunday through Saturday)
- **Effective from / Effective until** - the date range during which the template is active

Templates only apply within their effective date range. You can create multiple templates per employee to cover different days or different periods.

## Shift series

A shift series defines a repeating pattern that runs for a set duration. Series are useful for temporary assignments, rotations, or blocks of coverage.

Each series includes:

- **Employee** - the staff member the series applies to
- **Shift code** - the shift code for each occurrence
- **Frequency** - `daily`, `weekly`, or `biweekly`
- **Days of week** - which days within the frequency pattern are active
- **Start date / End date** - the bounds of the series
- **Max occurrences** - an optional limit on how many times the shift generates

## Applying a recurring schedule

Once you have templates or series configured, apply them to a date range to populate the grid with draft shifts.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Apply dialog">
    Click **Apply Recurring Schedule** in the schedule toolbar. This button is only visible if you
    have the `canApplyRecurringSchedule` permission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a date range">
    Choose the start and end dates for the period you want to fill. DubGrid will generate draft
    shifts for all matching templates and series within that range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Review the summary and click **Confirm**. The generated shifts appear on the grid as drafts.
    Publish them when you are ready to make them live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Permissions

Managing recurring shifts and series requires specific permissions granted by a Super Admin:

| Action                                        | Required permission         |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Apply recurring schedule to a date range      | `canApplyRecurringSchedule` |
| Create, edit, or delete day-of-week templates | `canManageRecurringShifts`  |
| Create, edit, or delete shift series          | `canManageShiftSeries`      |
