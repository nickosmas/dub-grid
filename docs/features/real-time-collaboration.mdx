---
title: "Working Together"
description: "Several people can edit the schedule at once. See who else is working and what happens when two edits meet."
---

Everyone viewing the schedule sees changes as they happen, without reloading. Drafts appear for other editors as they're saved, and a publish updates every open schedule. When you come back to a tab you left open, it catches up.

## Who else is editing

People who can edit the schedule see the other editors who have it open, as avatars in the toolbar: up to four, then a count of the rest. Hover over an avatar for the name, or open the list, **Editors online**, to see how many tabs or devices each person is using.

When another editor has a cell open, it shows their avatar and **Being edited by** their name. That's for information only: it never stops you from editing the same cell.

## When two edits meet

DubGrid doesn't lock cells. Instead, when you save a shift that someone else changed after you opened it, DubGrid tells you, for example **This shift changed in another tab or by another editor.**, and refreshes the schedule so you can see their change before making yours. Your save never silently overwrites someone else's. Reopen the shift panel and make your intended change again after the refresh.

## The same account in two places

If your account has the schedule open for editing somewhere else, such as another computer, a **Schedule open elsewhere** banner appears. To avoid conflicting unsaved changes, either:

- **Sign out this device**, or
- **End other schedule sessions**, which closes the schedule on those devices but leaves them signed in.

A tab whose session was ended says **Schedule session ended** and becomes read-only; changes it hadn't saved are lost. Select **Reload to start a new session** to keep working there.
