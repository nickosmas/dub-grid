---
title: "Draft and Publish"
description: "Schedule changes stay drafts until someone publishes them. How drafts, publishing, and discarding work."
---

Every change to the schedule starts as a draft. Staff keep seeing the published schedule until someone allowed to publish makes the drafts live, so nobody sees a half-finished schedule.

## Drafts

Only people who can edit the schedule see drafts. A changed cell is marked **Draft new**, **Draft changes**, or **Marked for deletion**. Drafts are saved as you make them, so closing the browser doesn't lose them, and every editor sees everyone's drafts.

While there are drafts in the period you're looking at, a **Draft changes** banner shows how many shifts are new, edited, or deleted and how many schedule notes changed. If other periods have drafts too, a second banner lists them so you can go to them.

## Publishing

1. Select **Publish** in the banner. If you can't publish, the button explains why.
2. The **Publish Schedule?** dialog lists the changes in this period by who made them: **Your drafts** first, then each other editor's. It warns you if an editor is on the schedule right now, since anything they haven't saved yet won't be included, and if coverage gaps remain.
3. Select **Publish**.

Publishing covers the period on screen. Drafts in other periods stay drafts until you publish those too.

After publishing, everyone viewing the schedule sees the change, and people with shifts in that period get a **Schedule updated** alert (except you). A **Recently published** bar shows what changed in the period you are viewing.

In the desktop one-week and two-week grids, **Highlight Changes** marks new, changed, deleted shifts and changed schedule notes until you hide them. The month view and phone-sized web view do not show this overlay, so switch to a desktop week view when you need to review individual changed cells.

Super Admins can always publish. Admins can publish when their Super Admin allows it, which is on for new Admins by default.

## Discarding drafts

Select **Discard** in the banner. The **Discard drafts?** dialog offers **Discard my drafts**, which removes only your own drafts in this period, or **Keep drafts**. When other editors also have drafts, a Super Admin also sees **Discard all drafts**.

Discarding leaves the published schedule as it is and doesn't touch drafts in other periods. Discarded drafts can't be restored.

## What staff see

Staff see only the published schedule. A period that has never been published shows **This period has not been published yet**. Approved shift requests are different: they change the published schedule straight away, without a draft. See [Shift requests](/features/shift-requests).
