---
title: "Shift Requests"
description: "Offer a shift for pickup, swap with a colleague, or ask for time off, and how managers approve requests."
---

Staff ask for schedule changes through requests instead of editing the schedule. There are three kinds:

| Request      | What it means                                                                               |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Pickup**   | You offer your shift for a teammate to take, or offer to cover a specific teammate's shift. |
| **Swap**     | You ask a teammate to trade shifts with you.                                                |
| **Time off** | You call off a shift you can't work.                                                        |

## Making a request

**On the web**, select your shift on the schedule (or right-click it) and choose **Drop shift**, then **Offer for pickup** or call off, or choose **Swap** and pick the teammate. **In the DubGrid app**, open your shift and use **Drop shift** or **Swap**.

You can cancel your own request while it's still open or awaiting approval, with **Cancel request**.

## How a request moves

| Status                | Meaning                                                                                                        |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Open**              | Waiting for a teammate: someone to take the pickup, or the teammate you asked to accept the swap.              |
| **Awaiting approval** | Waiting for a manager. A time-off request starts here.                                                         |
| **Approved**          | The change is on the published schedule.                                                                       |
| **Rejected**          | A manager declined it, or the teammate you asked declined.                                                     |
| **Cancelled**         | The person who asked cancelled it.                                                                             |
| **Expired**           | Nobody settled it within 72 hours of the request. Automatic expiry may not be available in every organization. |

- A **pickup** you offer stays open until a teammate claims it; then it waits for approval.
- A **swap** or a pickup offered to a named teammate waits for that teammate to **Accept** or **Decline** first.
- When a manager **Approves**, the change goes straight onto the published schedule, with no draft to publish. A manager who is part of the request themselves has it approved automatically.
- An approved time-off request leaves the shift open. When a scheduler fills it in a draft and publishes, the time off is settled.

## Where requests live

**On the web**, open **Schedule**, then **Tools** and **Requests**. The board has four tabs:

- **Available Shifts**: shifts offered for pickup that you can claim.
- **My Requests**: the requests you made or were asked to accept.
- **Approval Queue**: requests waiting for a manager, if you can approve requests. Editors who can't approve see **All Requests** instead.
- **History**: settled requests, filtered by type and outcome.

Your **Dashboard** also shows cover requests and available shifts.

**In the DubGrid app**, the **Requests** tab has **Available**, **All**, **Mine**, **Approval**, and **History**. You see the tab if you're on the schedule or can approve requests.

## Alerts

You get an alert when a request that involves you is made, approved, rejected, or expires.

## Who can approve

Super Admins can approve any request. Admins can when their Super Admin gives them permission to approve shift requests.
