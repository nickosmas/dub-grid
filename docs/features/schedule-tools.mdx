---
title: "Schedule Tools"
description: "Reuse a previous period, review large changes, print or export the schedule, and see publication history."
---

On the web, open **Schedule**, then **Tools**. The choices depend on what you are allowed to do and what your Organization has enabled. Every schedule-changing tool creates drafts, so review and [publish](/features/draft-publish) them when you are ready.

## Reusing a period

**Import Previous Schedule** copies the previous matching period into the period on screen as drafts. Review the import before confirming it. It does not publish anything, and it never changes a different period.

If your Organization uses recurring schedules, **Fill from Recurring Schedules** is a separate tool. It fills only empty slots from those patterns, from today on. See [Recurring shifts](/features/recurring-shifts).

## Reviewing a larger deletion

**Bulk Delete Entries** is available in one-week and two-week views on a desktop-sized screen. Select the entries you want to remove, then review them before confirming. Days before today can't be selected; [correct them](/features/schedule-grid) instead. The selected entries are marked for deletion as drafts. They remain on the published schedule until someone publishes the period.

To remove one entry instead, select its cell and use the remove button in the panel, such as **Remove shift** or **Remove shift and absence**. See [Schedule grid](/features/schedule-grid#editing-shifts).

## Printing and exporting

Choose **Print** to select the view, font size, and focus areas, then choose **Preview & Print**. When your organization shows full shift names, the printout covers one week. The printout leaves off anyone with nothing scheduled in the printed range, shows when it was printed, and includes a schedule-note legend, and a shift legend when shifts show as codes. Your browser can save the preview as a PDF.

Choose **Export CSV** to download the schedule currently on screen. Printing and exports may not be available in every Organization. See [Integrations and exports](/reference/integrations-and-exports) for the other exports and your calendar subscription.

## Authors and publication history

Choose **Authors** to show who made each visible draft change. This helps editors review work together before they publish.

Choose **Publish History** to review earlier publications that overlap the period you are viewing. Each change shows the shift before and after that publication, with the changed details highlighted. A shift on a day that had nothing shows as new. The schedule can also show the most recently published changes directly in the grid. Change highlights are for review only. They do not alter the schedule.

## What staff can use

Staff can read the published schedule and can use schedule-request actions that their access allows. Importing, bulk deletion, printing, CSV export, author labels, and publication history are management tools. If you do not see a tool, ask your Super Admin about your access.
