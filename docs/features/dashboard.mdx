---
title: "Dashboard"
description: "What the Dashboard shows each role, on the web and in the DubGrid app."
---

The **Dashboard** is a summary of the schedule for the period you choose. What it shows depends on your role. Use **This week** or the arrows at the top to look at another period.

## For Super Admins and Admins

A summary card at the top reads the health of the period: urgent coverage gaps, requests awaiting approval, a projected overtime alert, unsaved drafts, a period that hasn't been published, coverage requirements that aren't set up yet, or **Schedule health looks good**. Next to it are counts for **Coverage** and **Open gaps**, plus **Draft shifts** if you edit the schedule and **Pending approvals** if you approve requests.

Below it:

- **Your schedule**: your own shifts, if you're on the schedule.
- **Coverage by** focus area: how well each focus area is staffed.
- **Open shifts**: shifts that still need someone.
- **Recent activity**: what's changed lately. Accepted invitations appear here for people who manage staff.
- **Overtime watch**: people scheduled past their hours.
- **Review queue** (Admins): the requests and changes waiting on you.

Expand a card to see more, such as coverage by shift, open shifts, staff hours, and a breakdown of the schedule.

While your organization is being set up, a checklist on the Dashboard tracks what's left: your focus areas, departments, roles, certifications, shifts and jobs, employees, and your first schedule. It uses your organization's own labels.

## For Users

A User's Dashboard shows **Your Week** (or your schedule for the period), cover requests from teammates, shifts available to pick up, and your open requests. See [Shift requests](/features/shift-requests).

A User who manages a department but isn't on the schedule themselves goes straight to the **Schedule** instead of the Dashboard.

## In the DubGrid app

The **Home** tab is the app's dashboard. Super Admins and Admins see their own schedule, the period's health, drafts, open shifts, staff hours, and recent activity, and Admins also see their review queue; each opens into more detail. Everyone else sees their own schedule. Home is hidden for a User who manages a department but isn't on the schedule.
