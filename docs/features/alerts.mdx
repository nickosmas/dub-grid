title: "Alerts"
description: "Alerts tell you when something changes. Selecting one takes you to what it's about."
---

An alert is one line about something that changed: a schedule was published, a request was approved, your access changed. Selecting an alert marks it read and takes you to what it's about.

## Where alerts go

| An alert about                                           | Opens                                                                  |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A new shift request                                      | The approval list if you approve requests, otherwise your own requests |
| A request being approved, rejected, or expiring          | Your requests                                                          |
| A published schedule, a shift change, or a schedule note | The schedule at that date                                              |
| Recurring shifts                                         | The schedule                                                           |
| A person's record, status, or profile                    | That person, or **People**                                             |
| Invitations or leaving the organization                  | **People**                                                             |
| Your permissions or departments                          | Your own profile                                                       |
| Organization settings                                    | **Settings**                                                           |
| Billing and your subscription                            | **Settings > Subscription**                                            |
| Security, such as a password change or a new device      | Your profile's security section                                        |

## On the web

The bell at the top shows how many alerts you haven't read. It opens your latest 20; select one to go to it, **Mark all read**, or **View all alerts**.

The **Alerts** page has everything, with one toolbar:

- **Inbox** or **Archived**
- **All**, **Unread**, or **Read**
- search by title or message
- category: **Schedule**, **Shift requests**, **Membership**, **Account**, **Security**, **System**, and **Billing** for Super Admins
- priority: **Low**, **Normal**, **High**, or **Critical**
- **Newest first** or **Oldest first**
- **Select** to mark several read or unread, or archive them; **Mark all read** for everything

Each alert can also be marked read or unread, archived, or restored from the archive. An alert that isn't about anything in particular opens in place to show its details.

## In the DubGrid app

Open alerts with the bell on the **Schedule** or **Home** header. Filter by **All**, **Unread**, **Schedule**, **Requests**, **System**, or **Archived**, search, or **Mark all read**. Swipe an alert to mark it read or unread, or to archive or restore it.

Selecting an alert opens its subject in the app, such as the right date on the schedule, your requests, or a person. Alerts about billing or organization settings say **Open this on the web to see more.** Tapping a push notification opens its alert the same way.

## Choosing what you get

In your profile's notification settings you choose, by category, whether alerts appear in DubGrid and whether they're emailed to you, and in the app whether this phone gets push notifications. Security alerts can't be turned off. See [Notifications, privacy, and data](/account/notification-privacy-and-data-controls).
