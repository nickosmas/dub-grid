DubGrid is staff scheduling for care facilities. Managers build and publish the schedule on the web; everyone in your organization sees the published schedule, requests shift changes, and gets alerts on the web or in the DubGrid mobile app.

## Your organization

Your organization has its own people, schedule, settings, and address, such as `yourorg.dubgrid.com`. You sign in at that address, or enter your organization's subdomain (the `yourorg` part) on the DubGrid sign-in page.

Your organization describes its staff in its own words. The defaults are **Focus Areas**, **Certifications**, and **Roles**, and they can be renamed under **Settings > Labels**. These guides use the default names.

## The schedule

- **Views**: see **1 Week**, **2 Weeks**, or a **Month** at a time. Two weeks is the default on a wide screen.
- **Drafts**: every change is a draft until someone allowed to publish does so. A banner shows how many draft changes are waiting and offers **Publish** and **Discard**.
- **Working together**: while you edit, you see who else is on the schedule and which cell they have open. It never locks you out. If someone else changed a shift before you saved, DubGrid says so and refreshes the schedule instead of overwriting their change.
- **Recurring shifts**: a person's regular weekly pattern can fill the schedule for you.
- **Requests**: staff ask to pick up an open shift, swap a shift with a colleague, or call off, and a manager approves or declines.
- **Printing and calendars**: print a week, two weeks, or a month, and add your own shifts to Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Outlook.

## Who can do what

Everyone in your organization has one of three roles:

| Role            | What they do                                                                                              |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Super Admin** | Everything in your organization, including its settings, billing, and who can manage what                 |
| **Admin**       | The parts of scheduling and staff management their Super Admin has switched on for them, person by person |
| **User**        | Sees the published schedule, requests shift changes, and gets alerts                                      |

A person on the schedule who has no DubGrid account yet counts as a User. See [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles) for the full list of what an Admin can be given.

## Access is by invitation

There is no public sign-up. A Super Admin, or an Admin allowed to manage staff, invites each person by email. An invitation link works for 72 hours from when it was sent, and sending a new invitation replaces the old link.

## Web and mobile

Everyone can use the web app. The DubGrid mobile app covers the schedule, requests, people, alerts, the dashboard, and your own account. Building and publishing the schedule, reports, billing, Admin permissions, and organization settings stay on the web.
