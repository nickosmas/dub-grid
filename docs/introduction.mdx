DubGrid replaces spreadsheet-based scheduling with a modern web application designed for care facilities. Schedulers and admins manage staff rosters, build and publish shift schedules, and collaborate in real time — all from a single workspace.

## What DubGrid does

Each organization gets its own isolated workspace with its own staff roster, shift codes, focus areas (wings or departments), and schedule. You access your workspace at a unique subdomain — for example, `acme.dubgrid.com`.

## Product pillars

**Schedule grid** — A 1-week, 2-week, or month view of your schedule. Staff are listed by row, dates by column. Click any cell to assign a shift code; drag and drop to move shifts. Auto-calculated count rows appear at the bottom of each section.

**Role-based access control (RBAC)** — Four roles control what each person can do: Super Admin, Admin, and User within your organization, plus a platform-level Gridmaster role. Admins receive a configurable set of granular permissions set by the Super Admin.

**Real-time collaboration** — Changes sync across all open tabs and users instantly. Cell locks show when someone else is editing a cell, and presence indicators show who else is viewing the schedule.

**Draft/publish workflow** — Every edit is a draft until a Super Admin or permitted Admin publishes it. A banner shows the count of pending changes. Drafts are saved across browser sessions so nothing is lost.

## How organizations are structured

Each organization on DubGrid has:

- A **subdomain** (e.g., `acme.dubgrid.com`) that routes all users to the correct workspace
- A configurable **staff roster** with employee records, certifications, roles, and focus area assignments
- Custom **focus areas** (wings, departments, or sections) that organize the schedule grid
- Custom **shift codes** with colors, time defaults, and certification requirements
- A **Super Admin** who owns the workspace and manages users and settings

## Invite-only access

DubGrid does not have public registration. Every account is created through an invitation sent by a Super Admin or Admin with the appropriate permission. Invitation links expire after **72 hours**.
