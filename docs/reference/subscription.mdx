Your DubGrid subscription is managed through Stripe. This page explains each status and what action, if any, you need to take.

## Subscription statuses

| Status       | Meaning                                                           | Action required                                     |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| `active`     | Your subscription is current and in good standing.                | None, full access.                                  |
| `trialing`   | You are in a free trial period.                                   | None, full access until the trial ends.             |
| `past_due`   | A payment attempt failed. Your access may be restricted.          | Update your payment method in the billing portal.   |
| `canceled`   | Your subscription has ended.                                      | Contact support to reactivate your plan.            |
| `unpaid`     | Payment has failed repeatedly and the subscription is now unpaid. | Update your payment method immediately.             |
| `incomplete` | The initial payment did not complete.                             | Complete the pending payment in the billing portal. |

## Managing your subscription

You can cancel your plan, update your payment method, or view past invoices through the Stripe billing portal. Access the portal from **Settings → Billing**.

## Seats

Your subscription quantity matches your current member count. When members are added or removed, your seat count updates accordingly on the next billing cycle.
