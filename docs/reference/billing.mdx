---
title: "Billing"
description: "How DubGrid's seat-based billing works and how to subscribe."
---

DubGrid uses seat-based subscription billing. Your subscription quantity is based on the number of members in your organization.

## Your free trial

Every new organization gets a 14-day free trial. It starts automatically the first time a Super Admin signs in, so there is nothing to activate and no payment details to enter to begin.

When the trial ends, your organization needs an active subscription to keep full access. You can subscribe at any point during the trial.

<Note>
  Subscribing ends the trial and starts billing immediately. Stripe checkout does not add a second
  free period on top of your in-app trial, so subscribe when you are ready rather than on day one.
</Note>

## Who can access billing

Only Super Admins can view and manage billing. Admins, whatever other permissions they have been granted, cannot open billing settings or start a subscription.

## How to subscribe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open billing settings">
    Go to **Settings → Billing** in your organization dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the upgrade">Click **Upgrade** or **Subscribe**.</Step>
  <Step title="Complete checkout">
    You are redirected to the Stripe checkout page. Enter your payment details and confirm your
    purchase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Subscription activated">
    After a successful payment, your subscription is activated immediately and your organization
    gets full access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Seats

Your subscription quantity corresponds to the number of members in your organization. DubGrid calculates the seat count automatically when you start checkout.

<Note>
  Billing is handled through Stripe's secure checkout. DubGrid never stores your card details.
</Note>

<Tip>To check your current subscription status, see [Subscription](/reference/subscription).</Tip>
