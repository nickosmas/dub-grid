title: "Billing and Subscription"
description: "Your free trial, subscribing, seats, and what happens when billing needs attention."
---

Billing is for Super Admins only, on the web, under **Settings > Subscription**. It shows your subscription status, billable users, billed seats, and your trial or renewal dates.

## Your free trial

Your organization's free trial, usually 14 days, starts the first time a Super Admin signs in, with no payment details needed. Until then, other members see that the organization opens once an administrator finishes setup.

After setup, a **Your trial has started!** message shows how many days are left, with **Set up billing** or **Maybe later**. Near the end, usually in the last seven days, Settings reminds you that the trial is ending. When the trial ends, you usually have **3 extra days** to add billing before the organization is locked; Settings always shows your organization's exact dates. DubGrid Support can change how many days your trial has left, give you more extra days after it ends, end it early, or end it with no extra days; each change appears in your billing activity.

## When billing is paused

DubGrid Support can pause your organization's billing. While it's paused, everyone keeps full access, Settings shows **Billing paused**, your trial days don't count down, and a subscription isn't charged. When billing resumes, your trial gets back the days it had left. Pausing and resuming both appear in your billing activity.

## Subscribing

In **Settings > Subscription**, select **Start subscription**, enter a billing contact email, and complete checkout with our payment provider. Your subscription starts straight away; there's no second trial on top of the one you had. Billing is monthly, per seat.

**Seats** count your active staff, suspended staff, Away staff who are an Admin or Super Admin, and members who don't have a staff record; offboarded staff are never billed. **Who takes a seat** lists each group with how many of your billed seats it makes up, such as **25 of 31 seats**, and the total **Billed seats**. Suspended people keep their seat while they can't sign in, and Away people take one only while they can still manage; the page counts them without naming anyone. **Not billed** lists who doesn't take a seat. The number of seats is set when you subscribe; if your team grows or shrinks, **Seat difference** shows how many users are without a seat, or how many seats go unused.

Once you're subscribed, **Manage billing** opens our payment provider's billing page, where you manage your payment details and subscription. If your subscription ends, **Start subscription** comes back so you can subscribe again.

## When billing needs attention

| Situation                                                                                                                                                                             | What happens                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Trial ending soon**                                                                                                                                                                 | A reminder in Settings.                                                                                                                                 |
| **Trial ended**                                                                                                                                                                       | A reminder to add billing before the extra days run out, with the date.                                                                                 |
| **Payment needs attention** (a payment failed or is incomplete)                                                                                                                       | A notice asks you to update your payment details by a date, usually 14 days after the payment first needed attention. Everyone keeps access until then. |
| **Locked** (the trial and extra days are over, a payment stayed overdue past its date, DubGrid Support ended the trial with no extra days, or the subscription is canceled or unpaid) | The organization is on hold until billing is restored. Super Admins get an alert when it locks.                                                         |

While the organization is locked, Super Admins go straight to **Settings > Subscription** to fix it. Everyone else sees **Organization unavailable**: "This organization is on hold until an administrator sorts out its billing." with **Check again**, which also re-checks on its own.

Billing actions may not be available in every organization. Deleting your organization cancels its subscription; see [Activity log and deleting an organization](/configuration/organization-activity-and-controls).
