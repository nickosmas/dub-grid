title: "Billing and Subscription"
description: "Your free trial, subscribing, seats, and what happens when billing needs attention."
---

Billing is for Super Admins only, on the web, under **Settings > Subscription**. It shows your subscription status, billable users, billed seats, and your trial or renewal dates.

## Your free trial

Your organization's free trial, usually 14 days, starts the first time a Super Admin signs in, with no payment details needed. Until then, other members see that the organization opens once an administrator finishes setup.

After setup, a **Your trial has started!** message shows how many days are left, with **Set up billing** or **Maybe later**. Near the end, usually in the last seven days, Settings reminds you that the trial is ending. When the trial ends, you usually have **3 extra days** to add billing before the organization is locked; Settings always shows your organization's exact dates. DubGrid Support can change how many days your trial has left, end it early, or end it with no extra days; each change appears in your billing activity.

## Subscribing

In **Settings > Subscription**, select **Start subscription**, enter a billing contact email, and complete checkout with our payment provider. Your subscription starts straight away; there's no second trial on top of the one you had. Billing is monthly, per seat.

**Seats** count your active staff plus members who don't have a staff record. The number of seats is set when you subscribe; if your team grows or shrinks, **Seat difference** shows how many users are without a seat, or how many seats go unused.

Once you're subscribed, **Manage billing** opens our payment provider's billing page, where you manage your payment details and subscription. If your subscription ends, **Start subscription** comes back so you can subscribe again.

## When billing needs attention

| Situation                                                                                                                                     | What happens                                                             |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Trial ending soon**                                                                                                                         | A reminder in Settings.                                                  |
| **Trial ended**                                                                                                                               | A reminder to add billing before the extra days run out, with the date.  |
| **Payment needs attention** (a payment failed or is incomplete)                                                                               | A notice asks you to update your payment details. Everyone keeps access. |
| **Locked** (the trial and extra days are over, DubGrid Support ended the trial with no extra days, or the subscription is canceled or unpaid) | The organization is on hold until billing is restored.                   |

While the organization is locked, Super Admins go straight to **Settings > Subscription** to fix it. Everyone else sees **Organization unavailable**: "This organization is on hold until an administrator sorts out its billing." with **Check again**, which also re-checks on its own.

Billing actions may not be available in every organization. Deleting your organization cancels its subscription; see [Activity log and deleting an organization](/configuration/organization-activity-and-controls).
