DubGrid connects to other tools through calendar subscriptions and file exports. It doesn't offer a public API.

## Your shifts in your calendar

Each person can subscribe to their own published shifts and absences from a calendar app. The subscription covers 12 weeks from the start of the current week and updates on its own.

- **On the web**: in your profile's **Overview**, **Calendar subscription** adds your shifts to Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Outlook in one click, or **Copy link** for another app. Select **Create private link** first.
- **In the DubGrid app**: **Profile > Calendar subscription** adds them to your phone's calendar, Google, or Outlook, or shares the link.

The link is private: calendar apps read it without signing in. **Replace link** stops the old link at once; **Disable subscription** stops updates until you create a new link. Calendar subscriptions are for people on the schedule with an active staff record, and stop if the account is deactivated.

## Printing the schedule

On **Schedule**, **Tools > Print** opens **Print / Export Options**: choose the view (1 week, 2 weeks, or a month), the font size, and which focus areas, then **Preview & Print**. To save a PDF, choose your browser's print-to-PDF option. Printing may not be available in every organization.

## Exporting to a spreadsheet

| Export           | Where                                                                                                                                                          | Who                               |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| **The schedule** | **Schedule > Tools > Export CSV** exports what's on screen.                                                                                                    | People who can edit shifts        |
| **Staff**        | **People > Export**, on a desktop-sized screen. Includes each person's name, contact details, status, seniority, focus areas, certification, roles, and notes. | People who can view staff details |
| **Reports**      | **Export CSV** or **Export PDF** on any report. See [Reports](/features/reports).                                                                              | People who can view reports       |

Staff and report exports may not be available in every organization.
