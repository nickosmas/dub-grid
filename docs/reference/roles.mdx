title: "Roles and Permissions"
description: "The three roles in your organization, what each can do, and how roles change."
---

Everyone in your organization has one of three roles. Your role determines what you can see and change there.

| Role            | What they can do                                                                                                                               |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Super Admin** | Everything in your organization, including its settings, billing, roles, and each Admin's permissions                                          |
| **Admin**       | The scheduling and staff work their Super Admin switches on for them, person by person. See [Admin permissions](/reference/admin-permissions). |
| **User**        | See the schedule and the staff directory, make shift requests, and get alerts, on the web and in the DubGrid app                               |

A person on the schedule who doesn't have a DubGrid account yet counts as a User.

## Changing someone's role

A Super Admin changes a person's role on **People**: in the directory's **Access** column, or on the person's page. The change takes effect immediately. See [People](/staff/roster).

- Nobody can change their own role or permissions; ask another Super Admin.
- An organization's last Super Admin can't be demoted or removed. Make someone else a Super Admin first.
- Only a Super Admin can make someone a Super Admin. An Admin who can manage staff can invite people as a User or an Admin.
- Offboarding someone ends their access to your organization, and restoring them gives it back. Only a Super Admin can offboard or restore a Super Admin. See [People](/staff/roster).

## Away staff

Someone marked away keeps their role: they can still sign in and do everything their role allows, but they aren't scheduled. See [People](/staff/roster) for what Away and Offboarded mean.

## Settings

Most of **Settings** follows the permissions a Super Admin gives an Admin. **Organization Details**, **Shift Display Mode**, **Schedule Rules**, **Subscription**, **Activity Log**, and **Delete Organization** are for Super Admins only. See [Organization Details](/configuration/organization).
