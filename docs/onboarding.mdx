---
title: "Joining Your Team"
description: "Accept a DubGrid invitation, sign in on the web or the DubGrid app, and find your schedule."
---

This guide is for anyone invited to join your organization on DubGrid. All you need is the invitation email.

<Note>
  An invitation link works for **72 hours** from when it was sent, and it doesn't extend. If the
  page says **Invitation no longer valid**, ask whoever invited you to send a new invitation. Only
  the newest email's link works.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept the invitation on the web">
    Open the email, whose subject reads "You're invited to join *your organization* on DubGrid", and select **Accept invitation**. Accept it in a web browser, even if you plan to use the DubGrid app.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set your password">
    Your email address is filled in for you. Choose a password of at least 10 characters that includes a letter and a number, plus an uppercase letter or a symbol, then enter it again in **Confirm password**. Agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy and select **Set Password & Accept**.

    If you already use DubGrid with this email, enter your existing **DubGrid password** and select **Sign In & Accept** instead.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign in">
    After **You're all set**, DubGrid takes you to your organization's sign-in page.

    - **On the web**: sign in at your organization's address, such as `yourorg.dubgrid.com`, or enter the subdomain (the `yourorg` part) on the main DubGrid sign-in page and select **Continue**.
    - **In the DubGrid app**: enter your organization's subdomain, select **Continue**, then sign in with your email and password. **Switch organization** takes you back.

    If you use an authenticator app, enter its 6-digit code. Forgot your password? Select **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen.

  </Step>

<Step title="If your organization isn't open yet">
  Your organization may still be getting set up. **Setup in progress** means your administrator is
  still setting it up; you can get in as soon as they finish. **Organization unavailable** means
  your administrator still has to finish setup. Both screens let you check again.
</Step>

<Step title="Take the short tour">
  The first time you sign in, a short welcome tour introduces DubGrid. Admins also see an overview
  of what they can manage. You can skip it.
</Step>

  <Step title="Find your way around">
    **On the web**, the tabs across the top are **Dashboard**, **Schedule**, and **People**, plus **Reports** and **Settings** if you manage them. The bell shows your alerts.

    **In the DubGrid app**, the tabs are **Home**, **Schedule**, **Requests**, **People**, and **Profile**, depending on your role. The first time you open the app, it offers to turn on notifications so you hear about schedule changes and requests.

    What you can change depends on the role your organization gave you. Most staff see the published schedule and make [shift requests](/features/shift-requests).

  </Step>
</Steps>
