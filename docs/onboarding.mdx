---
title: "Joining Your Team"
description: "How to accept a DubGrid invitation and access your organization's schedule as a staff member."
---

This guide is for staff members who have received an invitation to join a DubGrid organization. You don't need any prior DubGrid experience, just your invitation email.

<Note>
  Invitation links expire **72 hours** after they are sent. If your link has expired, ask your
  administrator to send you a new invitation.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your invitation email">
    Look for an email from DubGrid in your inbox. It will include a link to accept your invitation. Click **Accept Invitation** to get started.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your account">
    On the **Accept Invitation** page:

    1. Your email address is pre-filled and cannot be changed.
    2. Enter a password in the **Password** field. Your password must be **at least 10 characters**. A strength meter shows how strong your password is. Aim for **Strong**.
    3. Re-enter your password in **Confirm password**.
    4. Check the box to agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
    5. Click **Set Password & Accept**.

    Once your account is created, you'll be redirected to your organization's sign-in page.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Check your inbox for a verification email and click the link inside to confirm your address.

    If the email doesn't arrive, go back to the login page, where you'll be prompted to resend the verification email (one resend allowed every 60 seconds).

  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign in">
    Navigate to your organization's subdomain, for example, `acme.dubgrid.com` - and sign in with the email and password you just created.

    If you're starting from `dubgrid.com/login`, enter your organization's subdomain first, then sign in on the next screen.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for organization assignment">
    After signing in for the first time, you may land on a **Pending Organization Assignment** page. This means your account has been created successfully, but your administrator still needs to link you to the organization in DubGrid.

    The page checks automatically every 15 seconds for up to 5 minutes. Once your administrator completes the assignment, the page refreshes and takes you to the schedule.

    You can also click **I've been invited, refresh** at any time to check manually.

    <Note>
      If you're still on this page after 5 minutes, your administrator may not have completed the invitation process yet. Contact them and ask them to finish assigning you to the organization in DubGrid.
    </Note>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Access the schedule">
    Once your organization assignment is complete, you'll land on the schedule. You can view the current schedule and navigate between weeks using the arrow buttons in the toolbar.

    Your access level depends on the role your administrator assigned to you. Most staff members have read-only access to the schedule by default.

  </Step>
</Steps>
