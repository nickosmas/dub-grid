## Get up and running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request a demo">
    Visit [dubgrid.com](https://dubgrid.com) and fill out the demo request form. Your organization
    will be provisioned with a dedicated subdomain (e.g., `acme.dubgrid.com`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept your invitation">
    A Super Admin invitation email is sent to you. Click the link to set your password and verify
    your email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete organization setup">
    The guided setup wizard walks you through adding employees, configuring focus areas, and setting
    up shift codes before you publish your first schedule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Go to **Settings → Users** to invite additional admins and staff members. Each invitee receives
    a 72-hour invitation link.
  </Step>
</Steps>
