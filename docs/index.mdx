DubGrid replaces the shared scheduling spreadsheet. Schedulers build the schedule in a web grid, changes stay drafts until someone publishes them, and staff see the published schedule, request pickups, swaps, and call-offs, and get alerts on the web or in the DubGrid mobile app.

## How an organization starts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request a demo">
    Fill in the form at **Request a demo** with your organization's name and size. The DubGrid team
    sets up your organization with its own address, for example `yourorg.dubgrid.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the invitation">
    The first Super Admin receives an invitation email. The link sets your password and joins you to
    the organization. It works for 72 hours from when it was sent, and only the newest invitation's
    link works.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your organization">
    The first time you sign in, a setup guide walks you through your organization's details and
    labels, its departments, roles, and certifications, and its shifts and jobs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Invite people from the last setup step or from **People** at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
