title: "Person Page"
description: "Everything about one person: their work details, record, access, schedule pattern, and history."

Each person has a page with everything about them. Open it by selecting their name on **People**. You need permission to view staff details; you see your own details on your profile instead.

Everything is on one page. **Back to People** returns to the directory.

## The header

Their name, with a status icon after it unless they're Active and a star for an Admin or Super Admin, and their employee number. **No app access** shows when they have no account or invitation. Depending on your permissions and the person, the header also offers **Send invitation** (they have an email but no account or invitation yet), and **Add to schedule** or **Add to management**.

Opened from **People**, the header also shows where they are in that list, such as **7 of 37**, with arrows to the previous and next person in the same order and filters.

## Beside the tabs

- **Contact**: their email and phone.
- **Work details**: employment type, focus areas, certification, roles, and internal notes.
- **Record**: when they were added and joined, their first day if one is set, their status and when it last changed with any note, whether they have an account, and when they were last active. The account line reads **Linked account**, **Invitation pending**, **Invitation expired**, **Not invited**, or **No email**.

If you can manage staff, **Edit** on Contact or Work details opens their details to change.

Above the tabs, a card says where their status stands, with **Change status** in it when you can change it, or a button named for the one change possible right now, such as **Offboard**. A [first day](/staff/roster#a-first-day) still ahead or a planned change has its own card beside it. See [People](/staff/roster) for what each status means. If their invitation is pending or expired, a banner says so, with **Resend** and **Revoke**. See [Invitations](/staff/invitations).

## Tabs

- **Overview**: their hours, average hours per week, shifts, and weeks with overtime for the period, this week day by day, and **What they work**, their shifts and jobs in the period as a chart, with a table view.
- **Shifts**: every shift in the period, newest first: the shift and job, its times, focus area and hours, and whether it's a draft, published or corrected, with what a draft replaces, a correction's reason, an absence beside it, and how many schedule notes it has. Filter by state, and **Export CSV** to download the rows shown.
- **Recurring**: their **Recurring schedule**, the weekly pattern for repeating assignments, and their shift preferences, with a link to change them on **Recurring Shifts**. You see it if you can view recurring schedules.
- **Access**: their role and the permissions it carries, and the departments they manage, if any. A Super Admin can change the role, choose which permissions an Admin has, and edit the departments; see [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles). Someone with no app access shows what's needed to invite them.
- **Activity**: profile edits, status changes, access changes, and invitations for this person. Super Admins see it, as do Admins who can view staff details.

On **Overview**, **Shifts**, and **Activity**, choose the period they cover: **Current week**, **Pay period**, **This month**, **Last 12 weeks**, or **Custom** dates, up to a year. Pay period needs your organization's pay period set in [Schedule Rules](/configuration/schedule-rules-and-display). Your browser remembers the period you chose.

## Editing a person

Edit their name, employment type, phone, email, internal notes, certification, focus areas, and roles. Someone on the schedule needs at least one focus area; removing all of a manager's focus areas takes them off the schedule while keeping their management access.

Taking someone off a focus area where they have shifts from today on asks first, with how many shifts and their dates. See [Taking someone off a focus area](/staff/roster#taking-someone-off-a-focus-area).

### Their email address

A person's email address is also the address they sign in with, so:

- Someone with an account must keep an email address.
- Changing it asks you to confirm your identity first. It signs them out everywhere and emails both the old and the new address about the change.
- Two people in your organization can't share an address.
- Changing the address of someone with a pending invitation asks whether to send a new invitation to the new address.

## Changes staff ask for

Staff who can't edit their own record change their phone number on their profile, and ask for name changes or account deletion there. Their requests appear under **People**, in **Requests**, for people who manage staff to approve or reject.

## In the DubGrid app

A person's screen in the app shows their access, contact details, staffing, assignments, and notes. People who manage staff can send, reissue, or revoke an invitation, add them to the schedule or to management, and mark them away, suspend them now, later or [until a date](/staff/roster#suspended-with-dates), offboard them now or after a [last day](/staff/roster#a-last-day), or bring them back now or [on a date](/staff/roster#restoring-on-a-date). A [first day](/staff/roster#a-first-day) still ahead shows on its own card, and a first day shows with their staffing.
