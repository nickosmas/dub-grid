title: "Person Page"
description: "Everything about one person: their work details, record, access, schedule pattern, and history."

Each person has a page with everything about them. Open it by selecting their name on **People**. You need permission to view staff details; you see your own details on your profile instead.

The page has three sections: **Profile**, **Overview**, and **Activity**. **Back to People** returns to the directory.

## The header

Their name, access level, email and phone, employment type if they're on the schedule, and their employee number.

## Profile

- **Work details**: employment type, focus areas, certification, roles, and internal notes. If you can manage staff, edit them in place.
- **Record**: when they were added and joined, their status and when it last changed with any note, whether they have an account, and when they were last active. The account line reads **Linked account**, **Invitation pending**, **Invitation expired**, **Not invited**, or **No email**.
- **Access**: their role and the permissions it carries. A Super Admin can change the role and choose which permissions an Admin has; see [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles).
- **Management departments**: the departments they manage, if any. A Super Admin can edit these.

Depending on your permissions and the person, you'll also see **Send invitation** (they have an email but no account or invitation yet), **Add to schedule** or **Add to management**, and **Activate** or **Deactivate**. See [People](/staff/roster) for what each status means.

If their invitation is pending or expired, a banner says so, with **Reinvite** and **Revoke**. See [Invitations](/staff/invitations).

## Editing a person

Edit their name, employment type, phone, email, internal notes, certification, focus areas, and roles. Someone on the schedule needs at least one focus area; removing all of a manager's focus areas takes them off the schedule while keeping their management access.

### Their email address

A person's email address is also the address they sign in with, so:

- Someone with an account must keep an email address.
- Changing it asks you to confirm your identity first. It signs them out everywhere and emails both the old and the new address about the change.
- Two people in your organization can't share an address.
- Changing the address of someone with a pending invitation asks whether to send a new invitation to the new address.

## Overview

This week's hours, average hours per week, shifts over the last 12 weeks, and weeks with overtime, followed by their details, their most common shifts and jobs, and their **Recurring schedule**, the weekly pattern for repeating assignments.

## Activity

**Activity** lists profile edits, status changes, access changes, and invitations for this person. Super Admins see it, as do Admins who can view staff details.

## Changes staff ask for

Staff who can't edit their own record change their phone number on their profile, and ask for name changes or account deletion there. Their requests appear under **People**, in **Requests**, for people who manage staff to approve or reject.

## In the DubGrid app

A person's screen in the app shows their access, contact details, staffing, assignments, and notes. People who manage staff can send, reissue, or revoke an invitation, add them to the schedule or to management, and activate, deactivate, or remove them.
