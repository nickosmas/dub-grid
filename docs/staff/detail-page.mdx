title: "Person Page"
description: "Everything about one person in three tabs: an overview, their schedule, and their activity."
---

Each person has a page with everything about them. Open it by selecting their name on **People**. You need permission to view staff details; you see your own details on your profile instead.

Everything is on one page. **Back to People** returns to the directory.

## The header

Their name, with a star for an Admin or Super Admin and a status icon after it unless they're Active. Under the name are their roles, focus areas and employment type, then their email and phone. Depending on your permissions and the person, the header offers **Send invitation** (they have an email but no account or invitation yet), **Add to schedule** or **Add to management**, and **Edit details** if you can manage staff.

Opened from **People**, the header also shows where they are in that list, such as **7 of 37**, with arrows to the previous and next person in the same order and filters.

Under the header, a card says where their status stands, with **Change status** in it when you can change it, or a button named for the one change possible right now, such as **Offboard**. A [first day](/staff/roster#a-first-day) still ahead or a planned change has its own card beside it. See [People](/staff/roster) for what each status means. If their invitation is pending or expired, a banner says so, with **Resend** and **Revoke**. See [Invitations](/staff/invitations).

## Tabs

- **Overview**: their hours, shifts, and average hours per week for the period; **This week**, or **This pay period** when the period is Pay period, shown day by day as on your dashboard; **Needs attention**, requests about them waiting on you, when there are any and you can review requests, each with **Review**; and **What they work**, their shifts and jobs in the period as a chart, with a table view. When their shifts come from more than one focus area, each shift names its focus area, since focus areas can share shift names. Under **About**:
  - **Work**: focus areas, certification, roles, and internal notes.
  - **Access**: their access level, how many permissions an Admin has, the departments they manage, if any, whether they have an account (**Linked account**, **Invitation pending**, **Invitation expired**, **Not invited**, or **No email**), and when they were last active. A Super Admin can **Edit** the access level and choose which permissions an Admin has; see [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles). Someone with an email and no app access offers **Send invitation**.
  - One line with their employee ID, when they were added and joined, their first day if one is set, when their status last changed, any planned change, and any status note.
- **Schedule**: their **Recurring schedule**, the weekly pattern for repeating assignments, with their shift and job preferences and a link to change them on **Recurring Shifts** (if you can view recurring schedules). Then **Shifts and time off** in the period, newest first, grouped by week: each shift with its job, times, focus area, what a draft replaces, a correction's reason, an absence beside it, and how many schedule notes it has; and each absence or time away with its dates and days. Drafts, corrections, and time off that's planned or happening now are marked. Show **Shifts and time off**, **Shifts only**, or **Time off only** from the dropdown, and **Export CSV** to download the shifts. Your organization's day off absence is left out unless you show it. When the period is **This month**, the month shows as a calendar instead of the list, each day with its shifts and absences in the schedule's colors and drafts outlined.
- **Requests**: their pickups, swaps, call-offs, and profile change requests sent in the period, newest first, with whether each is awaiting approval, approved, rejected, canceled or expired, and who decided. Ones **Waiting on you** have **Review**. Filter all requests, those waiting on you, or those decided or closed. The tab shows how many are waiting. You see it if you can review requests.
- **Activity**: profile edits, status changes, access changes, and invitations for this person, filtered by kind from its dropdown. Super Admins see it, as do Admins who can view staff details.

Choose the period the tabs cover: **Current week**, **Pay period**, **This month**, **Last 12 weeks**, or **Custom** dates, up to a year. Pay period needs your organization's pay period set in [Schedule Rules](/configuration/schedule-rules-and-display). Your browser remembers the period you chose. Older links to a Shifts, Time off, Recurring, or Access tab open the tab that holds it now.

## Editing a person

Edit their name, employment type, phone, email, internal notes, certification, focus areas, and roles. Someone on the schedule needs at least one focus area; removing all of a manager's focus areas takes them off the schedule while keeping their management access.

Taking someone off a focus area where they have shifts from today on asks first, with how many shifts and their dates. See [Taking someone off a focus area](/staff/roster#taking-someone-off-a-focus-area).

### Their email address

A person's email address is also the address they sign in with, so:

- Someone with an account must keep an email address.
- Changing it asks you to confirm your identity first. It signs them out everywhere and emails both the old and the new address about the change.
- Two people in your organization can't share an address.
- Changing the address of someone with a pending invitation asks whether to send a new invitation to the new address.

## Changes staff ask for

Staff who can't edit their own record change their phone number on their profile, and ask for name changes or account deletion there. Their requests appear under **People**, in **Requests**, for people who manage staff to approve or reject.

## In the DubGrid app

A person's screen in the app shows their access, contact details, staffing, assignments, and notes. People who manage staff can send, reissue, or revoke an invitation, add them to the schedule or to management, and mark them away, suspend them now, later or [until a date](/staff/roster#suspended-with-dates), offboard them now or after a [last day](/staff/roster#a-last-day), or bring them back now or [on a date](/staff/roster#restoring-on-a-date). A [first day](/staff/roster#a-first-day) still ahead shows on its own card, and a first day shows with their staffing.
