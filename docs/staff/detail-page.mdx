---
title: "Staff Detail"
description: "View an employee's full profile, schedule history, activity timeline, and reports."
---

Click any employee row in the roster to open their detail page at `/staff/[id]`. The detail page gives you a complete picture of a single employee, their profile, historical shifts, status changes, and hours data.

## Page header

The top of the page shows:

- **Avatar** - auto-generated from the employee's initials
- **Full name**
- **Status badge** - `active`, `benched`, or `terminated`
- **Certifications** - their certification abbreviation(s)
- **Quick actions** - bench, activate, or terminate the employee directly from this page

## Tabs

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Overview">
    The **Overview** tab shows the employee's complete profile information:

    - **Contact details** - phone number, email address, and any contact notes
    - **Certifications** - the certification levels assigned to this employee
    - **Focus areas** - the wings or sections they are assigned to
    - **Role tags** - the display roles your organization defines (e.g. `Supv`, `CN`, `Mentor`)

    Use this tab when you need to quickly look up contact information or confirm an employee's assignments.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Schedule">
    The **Schedule** tab shows a historical view of this employee's shifts.

    Use the **date range picker** to select any time window and review the shifts that were published for this employee. This is useful for checking past coverage patterns or verifying what an employee worked on a given date.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Activity">
    The **Activity** tab shows a chronological timeline of changes made to this employee's record, including:

    - Status changes (e.g. benched, activated, terminated)
    - Role or certification updates
    - Other notable events

    Each entry shows what changed, when it happened, and who made the change.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Reports">
    The **Reports** tab provides analytics for this individual employee:

    - **Hours history** - total hours worked over time
    - **Shift distribution** - a chart showing the breakdown of shift codes (D, E, N, etc.)
    - **Day-of-week patterns** - which days this employee typically works
    - **Focus area breakdown** - how their time is distributed across different wings or sections

    Use reports to identify scheduling patterns, balance workloads, or prepare for staffing reviews.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>
