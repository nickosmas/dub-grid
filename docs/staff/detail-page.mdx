title: "Person Page"
description: "Everything about one person in three tabs: an overview, their schedule, and their activity."
---

Each person has a page with everything about them. Open it by selecting their name on **People**. You need permission to view staff details; you see your own details on your profile instead.

Everything is on one page. **Back to People** returns to the directory.

## The header

Their name, with a star for an Admin or Super Admin and a status icon after it unless they're Active. Under the name are their roles, schedule areas and employment type, then their email and phone. Depending on your permissions and the person, the header offers **Add to schedule** or **Add to an administrative team**, .

Opened from **People**, the header also shows where they are in that list, such as **7 of 37**, with arrows to the previous and next person in the same order and filters.

Under the header, a card says where their status stands, with **Change staff status** in it when you can change it, or a button named for the one change possible right now, such as **Offboard**. A [first day](/staff/roster#a-first-day) still ahead or a planned change has its own card. See [People](/staff/roster) for what each status means.

If they can't sign in yet and aren't offboarded, an invite card sits beside the status card (under it on a narrow screen) and says where their invitation stands:

- **Not invited yet**, with **Invite**. Without an email on file, it asks you to add one and opens **Account** at the email field.
- **Invited** on a date, **hasn't joined**, with the day the invite expires, **Resend** and **Revoke**.
- **Invite expired** on a date, with **Resend**.

See [Invitations](/staff/invitations).

## Tabs

- **Account**: opens first, and you edit right on the page if you can manage staff. **Personal details** holds their name, phone, and email, and **Work** their employment type, internal notes, certification, schedule areas, and roles. Each card saves on its own with **Save** at its bottom (**Save & invite** on **Personal details** when saving also sends an invitation). Once a card has something to save, **Discard** joins it and both stay in view as you scroll through that card. If you can only view staff, you see **Work** as text.
  - **Access**: their access role, how many permissions an Admin has, their administrative teams, if any, their **Account**: whether they have one (**Linked account**, **Invitation pending**, **Invitation expired**, **Not invited**, or **No email**), and when they were last active. A Super Admin can **Change access** to set the access role and choose which permissions an Admin has (see [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles)), and **Edit** their administrative teams. For someone without a role, the access role says where their invitation stands, such as **Not invited yet**.
  - **Record**: their employee ID, when they were added and joined, their first day if one is set, when their status last changed, any planned change, and any status note.
  - **Delete account**: for a Super Admin, once someone has an account. See [Deleting their account](#deleting-their-account).
- **Schedule**: their hours, shifts, and average hours per week for the period, when they have any, with **What they work** under them: each shift and job they worked, its times, how many shifts and, when there's more than one kind, its share on a bar. When their shifts come from more than one schedule area, each shift names its schedule area, since schedule areas can share shift names. Then **This week**, shown day by day as on your dashboard, while the period on screen holds today. Under them, their **Recurring schedule**, the weekly pattern for repeating assignments, with their shift and job preferences and a link to change them on **Recurring Shifts** (if you can view recurring schedules, and only when they have recurring shifts or shift preferences). Then **Shifts and time off** in the period, shown first as a month calendar: each day with its shifts and absences in the schedule's colors, each shift with its job and, where schedule areas share its name, its schedule area on lines of their own, every card the same size, and drafts outlined. The period's days stand out and the rest of the month is dimmed; arrows step through the months picked dates cover. **Show as list** lists the period instead, newest first, grouped by week: each shift with its job, times, schedule area, what a draft replaces, a correction's reason, an absence beside it, and how many schedule notes it has; and each absence or time away with its dates and days. Drafts, corrections, and time off that's planned or happening now are marked. Show **Shifts and time off**, **Shifts only**, or **Time off only** from the dropdown, and **Export CSV** to download the shifts. Your organization's day off absence is left out of the list unless you show it.
- **Requests**: their pickups, swaps, call-offs, and profile change requests sent in the period, newest first, with whether each is awaiting approval, approved, rejected, canceled or expired, and who decided. Ones **Waiting on you** have **Review**. Filter all requests, those waiting on you, or those decided or closed. The tab shows how many are waiting. You see it if you can review requests.
- **Activity**: profile edits, status changes, access changes, and invitations for this person, filtered by kind from its dropdown. Super Admins see it, as do Admins who can view staff details.

The tabs cover one period at a time, starting with the current month. Beside the tabs, choose **Week**, **Pay period** (once your organization has set one), **Month** or **Year**; the arrows step back or forward one of them, and **Today** returns to the one holding today. To cover other dates, choose **Dates** and pick a range of up to a year; the arrows then step back to the period you chose. Older links to a Shifts, Time off, Recurring, or Access tab open the tab that holds it now.

## Editing a person

Their details are edited on the **Account** tab. Access and administrative teams have their own controls on the **Access** card, and **Add to an administrative team** in the header adds someone to one.

Edit their name, employment type, phone, email, internal notes, certification, schedule areas, and roles. Someone on the schedule needs at least one schedule area; removing all of someone's schedule areas takes them off the schedule while they stay on their administrative teams.

Taking someone off a schedule area where they have shifts from today on asks first, with how many shifts and their dates. See [Taking someone off a schedule area](/staff/roster#taking-someone-off-a-schedule-area).

### Their email address

A person's email address is also the address they sign in with, so:

- Once they have an account, they change it on their own profile. For your own, go to your profile too.
- Until then, you can change it here.
- If they've lost access to that email, **Move them to a new one** under the field moves their staff record to a new address and sends an invite to it, on the web and in the app. Their current sign-in email loses access to this organization, and they join with the invite at the same access role, keeping their shifts and history. They set a new password and two-factor. Their other organizations aren't affected. Only a Super Admin can move an Admin or Super Admin.
- Two people in your organization can't share an address or a phone number. If one belongs to someone else, the field says so and you can't save until it's changed.
- If someone changes their sign-in email to an address another staff record here already uses, their record keeps the old address and Super Admins get an alert naming the other record. Change that record's email, and theirs updates within a day.
- For someone who isn't invited yet, adding or changing their email offers **Invite to the app** under it, with their access role. It's ticked when the email is new, and **Save & invite** saves their details, then sends the invitation. With an email that hasn't changed, it stays unticked, so an ordinary save sends nothing.
- Changing the address of someone with a pending invitation offers **Send a new invitation to this email**, ticked. Leave it ticked and the new address gets a new link while the old one stops working; untick it and their invitation is canceled when you save.

### Deleting their account

A Super Admin can delete someone's account once they're Offboarded, from **Delete account** on the **Account** tab: type `DELETE ACCOUNT`, then confirm your identity. It deletes their account for good. Their staff record, shifts and history stay, and an email tells them.

If the account is also used outside your organization, it can't be deleted. Offboarding has already ended their access to yours.

## Changes staff ask for

Staff who can't edit their own record change their phone number on their profile, and ask for name changes or account deletion on the same page. Their requests appear under **People**, in **Requests**: people who manage staff approve or reject a name change, and a Super Admin decides an account deletion.

## In the DubGrid app

A person's screen in the app opens with their status, one row you tap to change it, with a planned change or a [first day](/staff/roster#a-first-day) still ahead as a line under it. Someone who can't change statuses sees only a planned time away, and no status for someone Active. **Access** follows: until they join, an **App invite** row that says where their invite stands, with **Send**, **Resend** or **Send again** (Send asks for their access role first, and with no email on file the row asks for one); then their access role, how many permissions an Admin has, and their administrative teams. A Super Admin taps **Access role** to change it, **Permissions** to choose an Admin's permissions (a View and an Edit switch for each group of permissions, as on the web), and **Administrative Teams** to change their teams. People who manage staff see an empty email or phone as **Add email** or **Add phone**, which opens the editor on that field. In the editor, **Move them to a new one** under a joined person's email moves them to a new address, as on the web, after you confirm your identity. People who manage staff can also revoke an invitation, add them to the schedule or to an administrative team, and mark them away, suspend them now, later or [until a date](/staff/roster#suspended-with-dates), offboard them now or after a [last day](/staff/roster#a-last-day), or bring them back now or [on a date](/staff/roster#restoring-on-a-date). A first day shows with their staffing.
