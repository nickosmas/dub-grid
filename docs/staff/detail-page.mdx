title: "Person Page"
description: "Everything about one person in three tabs: an overview, their schedule, and their activity."
---

Each person has a page with everything about them. Open it by selecting their name on **People**. You need permission to view staff details; you see your own details on your profile instead.

Everything is on one page. **Back to People** returns to the directory.

## The header

Their name, with a star for an Admin or Super Admin and a status icon after it unless they're Active. Under the name are their roles, focus areas and employment type, then their email and phone. Depending on your permissions and the person, the header offers **Add to schedule** or **Add to management**, and **Edit details** if you can manage staff.

Opened from **People**, the header also shows where they are in that list, such as **7 of 37**, with arrows to the previous and next person in the same order and filters.

Under the header, a card says where their status stands, with **Change staff status** in it when you can change it, or a button named for the one change possible right now, such as **Offboard**. A [first day](/staff/roster#a-first-day) still ahead or a planned change has its own card. See [People](/staff/roster) for what each status means.

If they can't sign in yet and aren't offboarded, an invite card sits beside the status card (under it on a narrow screen) and says where their invitation stands:

- **Not invited yet**, with **Invite**. Without an email on file, it asks you to add one and offers **Edit details**.
- **Invited** on a date, **hasn't joined**, with the day the invite expires, **Resend** and **Revoke**.
- **Invite expired** on a date, with **Resend**.

See [Invitations](/staff/invitations).

## Tabs

- **Overview**: their hours, shifts, and average hours per week for the period, with **What they work** under them: each shift and job they worked, its times, how many shifts and, when there's more than one kind, its share on a bar. When their shifts come from more than one focus area, each shift names its focus area, since focus areas can share shift names. Then **This week**, shown day by day as on your dashboard, and **Needs attention**, requests about them waiting on you, when there are any and you can review requests, each with **Review**. Under **About**:
  - **Work**: focus areas, certification, roles, and internal notes.
  - **Access**: their access level, how many permissions an Admin has, the departments they manage, if any, whether they have an account (**Linked account**, **Invitation pending**, **Invitation expired**, **Not invited**, or **No email**), and when they were last active. A Super Admin can **Change access** to set the access level and choose which permissions an Admin has (see [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles)), and **Edit** the departments they manage. For someone without a role, the access level says where their invitation stands, such as **Not invited yet**.
  - **Record**: their employee ID, when they were added and joined, their first day if one is set, when their status last changed, any planned change, and any status note.
- **Schedule**: their **Recurring schedule**, the weekly pattern for repeating assignments, with their shift and job preferences and a link to change them on **Recurring Shifts** (if you can view recurring schedules, and only when they have recurring shifts or shift preferences). Then **Shifts and time off** in the period, shown first as a month calendar: each day with its shifts and absences in the schedule's colors, every card the same size, and drafts outlined. The period's days stand out and the rest of the month is dimmed; arrows step through the months picked dates cover. **Show as list** lists the period instead, newest first, grouped by week: each shift with its job, times, focus area, what a draft replaces, a correction's reason, an absence beside it, and how many schedule notes it has; and each absence or time away with its dates and days. Drafts, corrections, and time off that's planned or happening now are marked. Show **Shifts and time off**, **Shifts only**, or **Time off only** from the dropdown, and **Export CSV** to download the shifts. Your organization's day off absence is left out of the list unless you show it.
- **Requests**: their pickups, swaps, call-offs, and profile change requests sent in the period, newest first, with whether each is awaiting approval, approved, rejected, canceled or expired, and who decided. Ones **Waiting on you** have **Review**. Filter all requests, those waiting on you, or those decided or closed. The tab shows how many are waiting. You see it if you can review requests.
- **Activity**: profile edits, status changes, access changes, and invitations for this person, filtered by kind from its dropdown. Super Admins see it, as do Admins who can view staff details.

The tabs cover one month at a time, starting with the current month. Use the arrows beside the tabs to step back or forward a month, and **Today** to return to the current month. To cover other dates, such as a pay period, choose **Dates** and pick a range of up to a year; the arrows then step back to whole months. Older links to a Shifts, Time off, Recurring, or Access tab open the tab that holds it now.

## Editing a person

**Edit details** in the header opens their details to change. Access and management have their own controls on the **Access** card, and **Add to management** in the header adds someone to management.

Edit their name, employment type, phone, email, internal notes, certification, focus areas, and roles. Someone on the schedule needs at least one focus area; removing all of a manager's focus areas takes them off the schedule while keeping their management access.

Taking someone off a focus area where they have shifts from today on asks first, with how many shifts and their dates. See [Taking someone off a focus area](/staff/roster#taking-someone-off-a-focus-area).

### Their email address

A person's email address is also the address they sign in with, so:

- Someone with an account must keep an email address.
- Changing it asks you to confirm your identity first. It signs them out everywhere and emails both the old and the new address about the change.
- Two people in your organization can't share an address.
- Changing the address of someone with a pending invitation asks whether to send a new invitation to the new address.

## Changes staff ask for

Staff who can't edit their own record change their phone number on their profile, and ask for name changes or account deletion there. Their requests appear under **People**, in **Requests**, for people who manage staff to approve or reject.

## In the DubGrid app

A person's screen in the app shows their access, contact details, staffing, assignments, and notes. People who manage staff can send, reissue, or revoke an invitation, add them to the schedule or to management, and mark them away, suspend them now, later or [until a date](/staff/roster#suspended-with-dates), offboard them now or after a [last day](/staff/roster#a-last-day), or bring them back now or [on a date](/staff/roster#restoring-on-a-date). Their status is one row you tap to change it, with a planned change, a [first day](/staff/roster#a-first-day) still ahead and their invitation as lines under it, and a first day shows with their staffing.
