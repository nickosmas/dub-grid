---
title: "Staff Roster"
description: "View, add, and manage every employee in your organization from the staff roster."
---

The staff roster at `/staff` is your central list of all employees. You can search, filter, and sort the table, and take actions on individual records, adding new hires, benching staff temporarily, or terminating employees who have left.

<Note>
  Add, edit, bench, and terminate actions require the `canManageEmployees` permission. Users without
  this permission can view the roster but cannot make changes.
</Note>

## What the roster shows

Each row in the table displays:

- **Name** - the employee's full name
- **Certification** - their primary certification abbreviation (e.g. `JLCSN`, `CSN III`)
- **Roles** - one or more role tags (e.g. `Supv`, `CN`, `Mentor`)
- **Focus areas** - the wings or sections they are assigned to
- **Status** - a badge indicating `active`, `benched`, or `terminated`

## Employee statuses

| Status         | Meaning                                                                                   |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Active**     | The employee is included in scheduling.                                                   |
| **Benched**    | Temporarily removed from scheduling without being terminated.                             |
| **Terminated** | Marked as no longer with the organization. Retained in the roster for historical records. |

## Search and filter

Use the **search bar** at the top of the toolbar to find employees by name. To narrow the list further, open the **filter panel** to filter by:

- **Status** - active, benched, or terminated
- **Focus area** - a specific wing or section
- **Certification** - a certification level defined in your organization's settings

You can also **sort** the table by seniority or name.

## Adding a new employee

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Add Employee modal">
    Click **Add Employee** in the top-right of the roster toolbar. The modal opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the employee's name">Fill in the employee's first and last name.</Step>
  <Step title="Set their certification and role(s)">
    Select their primary certification from the dropdown, then assign one or more role tags.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign focus areas and seniority">
    Choose which focus areas (wings/sections) the employee works in. Set their seniority number,
    lower numbers appear higher in the roster.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add contact information">Enter the employee's phone number and email address.</Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** to add the employee to the roster. They will appear as **Active** immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you're onboarding many employees at once, use **Bulk Import** to upload multiple records in a
  single step instead of adding them one by one.
</Tip>

## Editing an employee

Click anywhere on an employee's row to open the **Edit Employee** panel. Update any field, name, certification, roles, focus areas, seniority, phone, or email, then click **Save**.

## Benching an employee

Benching temporarily removes an employee from scheduling without terminating their record. Use this for staff on extended leave.

To bench an employee, open their row in the roster and select **Bench**. Their status badge changes to `benched` and they will no longer appear in the schedule grid.

To return them to active scheduling, select **Activate** from their row.

## Terminating an employee

Terminating marks an employee as no longer with the organization. Their record is retained for historical reporting.

To terminate an employee, open their row and select **Terminate**. You can add an optional note and the timestamp is recorded automatically. Their status badge changes to `terminated`.

<Note>
  Terminated employees remain visible in the roster when you filter by **Terminated** status. Their
  historical shifts and reports are preserved.
</Note>
