The **People** tab lists everyone in your organization. What you see and can change depends on your role: everyone sees the directory, and managers see more details and actions.

## The directory

**Directory** lists your colleagues with their focus areas, certification, and roles. Search by name, or by qualification. Your own row is marked **You**.

If you manage staff, the directory also shows each person's employment type, account, access level, and the date they joined, plus their email or phone, and you can search by name, email, or phone. If you can manage staff, selecting a name opens their [person page](/staff/detail-page).

Managers also get:

- **Status tabs**: **All**, **Active**, **Away**, and **Offboarded**, each with a count. Everyone else sees active staff only.
- **On Schedule** and **Management**, to switch between people on the schedule and people who manage a department but aren't scheduled, when your organization has management departments.
- **Filters**: qualifications, employment type, department, whether the person has an account, contact details, and access level.
- **Sort**: by seniority, name, or access level.
- **Reorder**: on the **Active** tab, sorted by seniority with no filters, drag people to change the seniority order and select **Save order**. **Discard** puts the rows back without leaving reorder mode.

The **Account** column says whether each person can sign in: **Linked** (they have an account), **Invited**, **Not invited**, or **No email**. People without an account still appear on the schedule.

## Adding people

Select **Add**. If you can add both kinds of people, choose **Scheduled staff** or **Management staff**.

**Add Staff Members** adds several people at once; use **Add Another Person** for each extra row. For each person enter:

- **First name** and **Last name** (required)
- **Employment**: full-time or part-time
- Their certification (optional)
- **Email** (optional; needed to invite them)
- At least one focus area

Then invite the people who should sign in. See [Invitations](/staff/invitations).

### Importing a list

On a desktop-sized screen, **Import** adds people from a `.csv` file of up to 500 rows. The file's columns are `first_name`, `last_name`, `email`, `phone`, `focus_areas`, `certification`, `roles`, and `notes`; only the names are required, and several focus areas are separated with semicolons.

Before importing, DubGrid checks for duplicates. A row that matches someone already on your roster by name, email, or phone, or another row in the file, is skipped. A row with a similar name but different contact details is flagged so you can double-check it. Importing may not be available in every organization.

## Changing someone's access

A Super Admin sets each person's access in the **Access** column: **User**, **Admin**, or **Super Admin**. The change takes effect immediately. You can't change your own access; ask another Super Admin.

Changing the access of someone whose invitation is still pending sends them a new invitation, and their old link stops working.

## Active, Away, Suspended, and Offboarded

| Status         | What it means                                                                           |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Active**     | On the schedule and able to sign in. Only active staff can be scheduled.                |
| **Away**       | Off the schedule for now. They keep their role and can still sign in.                   |
| **Suspended**  | Can't sign in on the web or in the app, but stays on the schedule and keeps their role. |
| **Offboarded** | No longer on staff: off the schedule and unable to sign in.                             |

A person's status shows in a card at the top of their record and the People panel, under their name. A planned time away, suspension or last day gets a second card of its own with **Edit plan**. In the People list and on their record, an icon after the name marks anyone who isn't plainly Active: a lock for Suspended, a moon for Away, a calendar for time away, a suspension or a last day that's planned, and a person with a minus sign for Offboarded. Point at the icon to read what it means.

The card's button is the likely next step. For someone suspended it's **Reinstate**, for someone away **Mark active**, and for someone offboarded **Restore**; each opens a confirmation for that change alone. The **⋯** menu beside it names each other change, such as **Mark away** or **Offboard**, for someone away **Change return date**, and for someone suspended **Set end date** or **Change end date**, each with its own confirmation. Someone active has **Change status** instead, or, when only one change is possible for them right now, a button named for it, such as **Offboard**.

To change someone's status, select **Change status** in that card. It shows their status now and lists every other status; pick one, add an optional reason where it asks, and confirm. A status they can't move to right now is still listed, grayed out, with the reason in place of its description, such as a planned away you change with **Edit plan**. The title and the button name what happens, such as **Mark away**, **Suspend**, **Reinstate** or **Restore**, and the confirmation lists what changes apart from what stays the same. Only someone on the schedule can be marked away, and only someone with an account can be suspended, since suspending only stops sign-in. Suspending someone signs them out everywhere at once, emails them that their access is suspended, and alerts your other Super Admins. Their open requests close, and a pickup they claimed goes back up for others. They stay on the schedule with a lock beside their name, since they won't see their shifts in the app until you reinstate them. You can also select several people and use **Mark away**, **Mark active**, **Restore**, or **Offboard** together.

When someone is marked away or offboarded:

- An offboarded person is signed out everywhere. An Away person stays signed in.
- From today on, their draft shifts are cleared and their published shifts are marked for removal at the next publish. Their recurring schedules and repeating shifts stop, and their open requests expire.
- Their past schedule is kept. Bringing them back doesn't restore what was cleared.

### Away with dates

When you mark someone away, you can give a **Return date**: the first day they're back. Until then they show as **Away until** that date. You can change or clear it, and its absence, later from **Change status**.

- Only the days before the return date are cleared, and they can be scheduled again from that date, even while still Away.
- Their recurring schedules pause instead of stopping, and fill again from the return date.
- On the return date they're set back to **Active** automatically, in your organization's time zone.

With a return date, you can also pick an **Absence**, such as Vacation. Every day from today until they're back then shows that absence in the draft, in place of any shift, and it's published with the rest of the period. Moving the return date, changing or clearing the absence, or marking them active early updates those days to match.

Time away can also start later. Pick a **Starts** date up to a year ahead and confirm with **Schedule away**:

- They stay **Active** until then, and their record shows **Away from** that date. They can be scheduled as usual before it.
- From the start date, their days can't be scheduled. Those days are cleared in the draft straight away, under the absence if you picked one, and open requests for them are closed.
- On the start date they're marked **Away** automatically, with the return date and absence you chose.
- To change the dates or the absence, select **Edit plan** and then **Save changes**. To start the time away today instead, clear **Starts** and confirm with **Mark away**. **Cancel planned away** puts back the days that held the absence, and keeps the rest cleared.
- Suspending or offboarding them, or scheduling a suspension, cancels the planned time away.

### A last day

When someone is leaving, pick **Offboard** and give their **Last day**, today or up to a year ahead, then confirm with **Set last day**:

- They stay **Active** through their last day, and show **Leaving** with that date. They can be scheduled as usual up to and on it.
- The days after it can't be scheduled. Their shifts after it are cleared in the draft straight away, and open requests for those days are closed.
- The day after their last day, in your organization's time zone, they're offboarded automatically, just as if you'd selected **Offboard** then.
- To move the last day, select **Edit plan** and then **Save changes**. To offboard them today instead, clear **Last day** and confirm with **Offboard**. **Cancel last day** keeps them Active; shifts already cleared stay cleared.
- Someone has one planned change at a time: a last day replaces a planned time away or suspension, and marking them away, scheduling time away or suspending them cancels the last day. Each confirmation says so.
- Only someone Active can have a last day. Someone away or suspended is offboarded straight away.

### Suspended with dates

When you suspend someone, you can give an **Ends** date: the first day they have access again. Until then they show as **Suspended until** that date, and when they try to sign in they're told their access is suspended until then. On that date, in your organization's time zone, they're reinstated automatically, just as if you'd selected **Reinstate**. For someone suspended, **Set end date** or **Change end date** in the card's **⋯** menu changes or clears it.

A suspension can also start later. Pick a **Starts** date up to a year ahead and confirm with **Schedule suspension**:

- They keep their access until then, and their record shows **Suspended from** that date. Nothing changes on the schedule: a suspended person stays on it.
- On the start date they're suspended automatically, just as if you'd selected **Suspend** then: signed out everywhere, emailed, your other Super Admins alerted, and their open requests closed. It ends on the **Ends** date if you gave one.
- To change the dates, select **Edit plan** and then **Save changes**. To suspend them today instead, clear **Starts** and confirm with **Suspend**. **Cancel planned suspension** keeps their access as it is.
- It's one planned change like the others: it replaces a planned time away or last day, and marking them away or offboarding them cancels it.

You can't change your own status, and an organization's only Super Admin can't be offboarded.

## Who can do what

| To                                               | You need                         |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------- |
| See the directory                                | Everyone                         |
| See contact details, account, and access         | Permission to view staff details |
| Add, import, edit, or change the status of staff | Permission to manage staff       |
| Change someone's access, or add management staff | Super Admin                      |

Exporting staff data is available to people who can view staff details, and may not be available in every organization.

## In the DubGrid app

The app's **People** tab has a **Schedule** and **Management** switch, search, filters, and sorting. Managers can add people, approve or reject profile requests, and change a person's status or invitation from their page.
