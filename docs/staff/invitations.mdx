title: "Invitations"
description: "Invite people to sign in to your organization, and reissue or revoke an invitation."
---

People sign in to DubGrid only by invitation. You invite someone who's already on your roster, or a manager you add as you invite them. There's no public sign-up.

## Who can invite

A Super Admin can invite anyone and give them any access level. An Admin who can manage staff can invite people from their person page, as a **User** or **Admin**. Only a Super Admin can invite another Super Admin.

## Sending an invitation

- **Someone on your roster**: open their [person page](/staff/detail-page) and select **Send invitation**. It's sent to the email address in their profile; choose their access level and select **Send Invitation**. Super Admins can also invite from the directory, one person or several at once.
- **A manager who isn't on the schedule**: on **People**, select **Add**, then **Management staff**. Enter their name, email, optional phone, the departments they'll manage, and their access level, and select **Send Invitation**.
- **During setup**: the first Super Admin can invite people from the setup guide's **Invite Team** step.

You can't invite an address that's already part of your organization. If the address already has a DubGrid account at another organization, the person joins yours when they accept, using their existing password.

## What the invitee gets

An email with the subject "You're invited to join _your organization_ on DubGrid" and an **Accept invitation** button. It says exactly when the link expires, in your organization's time zone. It doesn't say who sent it.

The link works for **72 hours** from when it was sent and doesn't extend. The invitee sets a password and agrees to the terms, then signs in; see [Joining your team](/onboarding).

## Reissuing and revoking

On the person's page, a banner shows a pending or expired invitation:

- **Reinvite** sends a new link and confirms **Reissue Invitation?** first. The old link stops working immediately; the invitation keeps its email and access level. A reissued email's subject reads "Your new invitation to join _your organization_ on DubGrid".
- **Revoke** confirms **Revoke Invitation?**, then the link stops working immediately.

Inviting the same address again while an invitation is pending reissues it, as long as the access level matches. Changing a pending invitation's access level in the **Access** column also sends a new link.

If you send several invitations to the same address in a short time, DubGrid asks you to wait a few minutes before trying again.

## When an invitation expires

An expired link stops working. The person's page then shows **Invitation expired** with **Reinvite**, and the person who sent it and the Super Admins get an alert, which may not be available in every organization.

## In the DubGrid app

People who manage staff can send, reissue, and revoke invitations from a person's screen. Invitations sent from the app give User access.
