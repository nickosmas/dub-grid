---
title: "Invitations"
description: "Invite employees to create their DubGrid account — no public sign-up is available."
---

DubGrid is invite-only. There is no public sign-up page. To give an employee access to your organization, you send them an invitation from **Settings → Users**. The invitation email contains a unique link they use to create their account.

<Warning>
  Invitation links expire after 72 hours. If a link expires before the employee accepts it, go to
  **Settings → Users** and send a new invitation.
</Warning>

## How to invite an employee

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings → Users">
    In the left sidebar, navigate to **Settings**, then select **Users**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the employee row">
    Locate the employee you want to invite in the users table. The employee must already exist as a
    record in your staff roster.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite">
    Click the **Invite** button on their row. DubGrid sends an invitation email to the employee's
    email address on file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the employee to accept">
    The employee receives the email, clicks the invitation link, and completes account setup. Once
    they accept, their account is linked to their employee record automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign their org role">
    After the employee sets their password and verifies their email, they land on a waiting page. Go
    to **Settings → Users** and assign them an org role to grant access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Each invitation is tied to the employee's record in the roster. When they accept, their DubGrid
  account is automatically connected to their employee profile — no manual linking is required.
</Note>

## What the employee receives

When you send an invitation, the employee gets an email with a **Accept Invitation** button. The email includes your organization name and the inviter's name.

The employee then:

1. Clicks the invitation link
2. Sets their password (minimum 10 characters)
3. Accepts the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy
4. Verifies their email address
5. Lands on an onboarding waiting page until you assign their org role

## Resending an invitation

If the employee didn't receive the email or the link expired, go back to **Settings → Users**, find their row, and click **Invite** again. A fresh 72-hour link is sent to their email address.
