---
title: "Departments and Focus Areas"
description: "Organize the schedule into departments and focus areas, and set up departments for people who manage but aren't scheduled."
---

**Settings > Departments** decides how your schedule is organized. It has two kinds of department:

- **Scheduled Departments** appear on the schedule. Each one has one or more **focus areas** that group staff on the grid, for example North Wing and Memory Care in a nursing department.
- **Management Departments** are for people who use DubGrid but don't appear on the schedule, such as HR, Reception, or Finance.

Your organization may call focus areas something else; see [Labels](/configuration/terminology).

## Setting them up

If you can manage focus areas, select **Edit**, then:

- **Add a department** under either heading, with a name of up to 80 characters. Department names must be unique.
- **Add focus areas** to a scheduled department with **+ Add** followed by your focus-area label. A department with a single focus area offers **+ Split into** your focus areas instead. Focus area names must be unique within their department.
- **Reorder** departments and focus areas by dragging them.

A scheduled department without focus areas can't be scheduled yet, and says so.

## Removing one

Removing a department or focus area that's in use asks **Archive** first, and offers **Rename Instead** if you only meant to change its name. Archiving keeps its history. One that nothing refers to asks **Delete** and is removed.

## On the schedule

- The grid groups people by scheduled department, then by focus area. See [Schedule grid](/features/schedule-grid).
- Someone assigned to several focus areas appears in each of those sections.
- The schedule toolbar filters to **All** or one focus area.
- Management departments never appear on the schedule. People in them manage the departments assigned to them on their [person page](/staff/detail-page).

## Who can do what

People with permission to view focus areas or organization labels can see this section. Changing it needs permission to manage focus areas. Super Admins can do both.
