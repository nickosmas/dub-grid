Both sections are for Super Admins only.

## Schedule Rules

**Settings > Schedule Rules** holds five organization-wide rules. Saving any of them asks you to confirm **Save Schedule Rule**, since it affects everyone.

| Rule                                 | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Block overlapping shifts**         | Nobody can be given two shifts that overlap.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Biweekly pay period start date**   | Set one date and every two-week view of the schedule lines up with that repeating pay period. Leave it blank to line two-week views up with calendar weeks.                                                                                            |
| **Mentored coverage credit**         | How much a mentored person counts toward coverage: **Counts fully** (the default), **Counts as half**, or **Does not count**.                                                                                                                          |
| **Day off absence**                  | The absence that means a regular day off. Auto-build writes it into the empty days of everyone it schedules. Until you save a choice, it is picked from an absence named Off, Day off or Rest (or coded X); choose **None** to leave empty days empty. |
| **Show coverage shortages to staff** | **When it fits their availability**, **Always show**, or **Hidden**. Schedulers always see shortages.                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Show call-off vacancies to staff** | The same three choices, for shifts left open by a call-off.                                                                                                                                                                                            |

## Shift Display Mode

**Settings > Shift Display Mode** chooses how shifts appear on the schedule:

- **Short Codes**: abbreviations such as D, EVE, or N in the schedule, the month view, and recurring schedules. Denser.
- **Full Names**: each shift's full name. Easier to read, but cells take more room.

Two more options:

- **Shift detail hover cards** (on by default): hovering over a shift on the schedule shows its full details, times, assignment, and status.
- **Use compact role and certification labels** (off by default): show the abbreviations you've set instead of full role and certification names. See [Roles, certifications, and schedule notes](/configuration/roles-certifications-and-indicators).

### What staff see after a publish

Two switches, both on by default, decide what staff see about a published change. People who can edit or publish the schedule, and Super Admins, always see both.

- **What changed**: staff see what each changed shift was before. Turned off, staff still see that a shift is new, changed or removed, but not what it was.
- **Who published**: staff see who published the schedule. Turned off, staff see only when it was published, and the names of people who edited shifts are hidden too.

With **Full Names**, shift codes aren't needed, and codes for jobs and absences become optional. Select **Save** to apply, or **Cancel** to undo your changes.
