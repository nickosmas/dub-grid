---
title: "Departments and Areas"
description: "Organize the schedule into departments and areas, and set up administrative teams for people who aren't scheduled."
---

**Settings > Departments** decides how your schedule is organized. It has two sections:

- **Departments** appear on the schedule. Each one has one or more **areas** that group staff on the grid, for example North Wing and Memory Care in a nursing department.
- **Administrative Teams** are for people who use DubGrid but don't appear on the schedule, such as HR, Reception, or Finance.

Your organization may call areas something else, such as Units or Wings; see [Labels](/configuration/terminology).

## Setting them up

If you can manage areas, select **Edit**, then:

- **Add a department** with **+ Add Department**, or a team with **+ Add Team**, with a name of up to 80 characters. Department and team names must be unique.
- **Add areas** to a department with **+ Add** followed by your area label. A department with a single area offers **+ Split into** your areas instead. Area names must be unique within their department.
- **Reorder** departments, teams, and areas by dragging them.

A department without areas can't be scheduled yet, and says so.

## Removing one

Removing a department, team, or area that's in use asks **Archive** first, and offers **Rename Instead** if you only meant to change its name. Archiving keeps its history. One that nothing refers to asks **Delete** and is removed.

## On the schedule

- The grid groups people by department, then by area. See [Schedule grid](/features/schedule-grid).
- Someone assigned to several areas appears in each of those sections.
- The schedule toolbar filters to **All** or one area.
- Administrative teams never appear on the schedule. Someone can be on a team and on the schedule at once; their teams are set on their [person page](/staff/detail-page).

## Who can do what

People with permission to view areas or organization labels can see this section. Changing it needs permission to manage areas. Super Admins can do both.
