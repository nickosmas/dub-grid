---
title: "Departments and Schedule Areas"
description: "Organize the schedule into departments and schedule areas, and set up administrative teams for people who aren't scheduled."
---

**Settings > Departments** decides how your schedule is organized. It has two sections:

- **Departments** appear on the schedule. Each one has one or more **schedule areas** that group staff on the grid, for example North Wing and Memory Care in a nursing department.
- **Administrative Teams** are for people who use DubGrid but don't appear on the schedule, such as HR, Reception, or Finance.

Your organization may call schedule areas something else, such as Units or Wings; see [Labels](/configuration/terminology).

## Setting them up

If you can manage schedule areas, select **Edit**, then:

- **Add a department** with **+ Add Department**, or a team with **+ Add Team**, with a name of up to 80 characters. Department and team names must be unique.
- **Add schedule areas** to a department with **+ Add** followed by your schedule area label. A department with a single schedule area offers **+ Split into** your schedule areas instead. Schedule area names must be unique within their department.
- **Reorder** departments, teams, and schedule areas by dragging them.

A department without schedule areas can't be scheduled yet, and says so.

## Removing one

Removing a department, team, or schedule area that's in use asks **Archive** first, and offers **Rename Instead** if you only meant to change its name. Archiving keeps its history. One that nothing refers to asks **Delete** and is removed.

## On the schedule

- The grid groups people by department, then by schedule area. See [Schedule grid](/features/schedule-grid).
- Someone assigned to several schedule areas appears in each of those sections.
- The schedule toolbar filters to **All** or one schedule area.
- Administrative teams never appear on the schedule. Someone can be on a team and on the schedule at once; their teams are set on their [person page](/staff/detail-page).

## Who can do what

People with permission to view schedule areas or organization labels can see this section. Changing it needs permission to manage schedule areas. Super Admins can do both.
