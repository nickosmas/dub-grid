---
title: "Shifts, Jobs, and Absences"
description: "Define your shifts, the jobs people do on them, and the absences that replace them."
---

DubGrid comes with no built-in shifts, jobs, or absences: your organization adds its own. The setup guide asks for at least one shift and one job before you start. All three live under **Settings > Scheduling**.

- A **shift** is a block of time in a focus area, such as Day, Evening, or Night.
- A **job** is what someone does, such as Supervisor, Mentor, or Nurse. Scheduled jobs go with a shift; general jobs stand on their own.
- An **absence** replaces working, such as PTO or a sick day, and doesn't count toward coverage.

## Shifts

Shifts are set per focus area. Select **+ Add Shift** in a focus area and give it:

| Field                 | Notes                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Name**              | Required, up to 50 characters, unique within the focus area.                                                                                   |
| **Code**              | Up to 8 characters, the short form shown on the schedule. Made from the name if you leave it blank. Hidden when the schedule shows full names. |
| **Color**             | Choose from the presets.                                                                                                                       |
| **Start** and **End** | The default times. People can have different times on a given day.                                                                             |
| **Break (min)**       | Optional, up to 480 minutes. The shift shows its gross, break, and net hours.                                                                  |

## Jobs

**Settings > Jobs** has up to three groups:

- **Scheduled Jobs**, worked on a shift.
- **General Jobs**, worked without a shift, with a default length or fixed default times (fixed times win).
- **Default Shift Job**, used when **Shift-only assignments** is on, letting staff be scheduled with just a shift and no job. It's hidden on the schedule but still decides where the shift goes, who qualifies, and its times and colors.

For each job:

| Setting                | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Job name**           | Up to 50 characters, unique.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Grid abbreviation**  | Up to 6 characters, unique. Required when the schedule shows short codes.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Color**              | Choose from the presets.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Placement              | The scheduled departments, focus areas, and shifts it's worked in.                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Per-shift Settings** | A different color or times for particular shifts.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Eligibility**        | Which roles and certifications can work it. **Require both** needs a matching role and certification; **Allow either** accepts one or the other. Leave a list empty to let anyone through on that side. Only roles set to count for job eligibility appear. |

A scheduled job needs a scheduled department with a focus area and a shift to go in. Jobs are listed by how much qualification they need.

## Absences

**Settings > Absence Types** lists the off-day and call-off labels your schedulers use. Each has:

- a **Code / label** of up to 6 characters, unique, required when the schedule shows short codes (for example PTO)
- a full name of up to 50 characters
- a color

## Removing one

Removing a shift, job, or absence that's in use archives it: its history stays, but it can't be used for new scheduling. One that nothing refers to is deleted.

## Who can do what

People with permission to view shifts and jobs can see these sections. Changing them, including turning shift-only assignments on or off, needs permission to manage shifts and jobs. Super Admins can do both.
