---
title: "Shifts, Jobs, and Absences"
description: "Define your shifts, the jobs people do on them, and the absences that replace them."
---

DubGrid comes with no built-in shifts, jobs, or absences: your organization adds its own. The setup guide asks for at least one shift and one job before you start. All three live under **Settings > Scheduling**.

- A **shift** is a block of time in a schedule area, such as Day, Evening, or Night.
- A **job** is what someone does, such as Supervisor, Mentor, or Nurse. Scheduled jobs go with a shift; general jobs stand on their own.
- An **absence** replaces working, such as PTO or a sick day, and doesn't count toward coverage.

## Shifts

Shifts are set per schedule area. Select **Add Shift** in a schedule area and give it:

| Field                 | Notes                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Name**              | Required, up to 50 characters, unique within the schedule area.                                                                                |
| **Code**              | Up to 8 characters, the short form shown on the schedule. Made from the name if you leave it blank. Hidden when the schedule shows full names. |
| **Color**             | Choose from the presets.                                                                                                                       |
| **Start** and **End** | The default times. People can have different times on a given day.                                                                             |
| **Break (min)**       | Optional, up to 480 minutes. The shift shows its gross, break, and net hours.                                                                  |

## Jobs

**Settings > Jobs** has three cards:

- **Allow shifts without a job** lets staff be scheduled with just a shift, like Day Shift. While it's on, its **Shift only** row decides where those shifts go, who qualifies, and their times and colors. The schedule shows the shift alone.
- **Scheduled Jobs**, worked on a shift.
- **General Jobs**, worked without a shift, with a default length or fixed default times (fixed times win).

Each row names the departments, schedule areas, and shifts a job is worked in: up to three of each, then how many more.

### Turning off shifts without a job

Turning **Allow shifts without a job** off stops new ones and keeps the ones you have. The confirmation counts what stays.

- Shifts already scheduled without a job stay on the schedule, count toward coverage, and can still be edited, swapped, or called off. Change them if they need a job.
- Nobody new is scheduled on one: not from the shift picker, Auto-build, a recurring schedule, copy and paste, dragging, or importing a previous schedule. An open shift that only a shift without a job could fill isn't offered.
- Recurring days that use one wait, with a note on the Recurring Shifts page and the person's schedule, until you allow them again or give them a job.
- Coverage requirements for one stay in **Settings > Coverage** with a note. They still count, and Auto-build won't fill them.

Turning it back on picks everything up again.

For each job:

| Setting                | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Job name**           | Up to 50 characters, unique.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Grid abbreviation**  | Up to 6 characters, unique. Required when the schedule shows short codes.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Color**              | Choose from the presets.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Placement              | The departments, schedule areas, and shifts it's worked in.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Per-shift Settings** | A different color or times for particular shifts, with a preview of each as the schedule shows it.                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Eligibility**        | Which roles and certifications can work it. **Require both** needs a matching role and certification; **Allow either** accepts one or the other. Leave a list empty to let anyone through on that side. Only roles set to count for job eligibility appear. |

A scheduled job needs a department with a schedule area and a shift to go in. Jobs are listed by how much qualification they need.

## Absences

**Settings > Absence Types** lists the absence and call-off labels your schedulers use. Each has:

- a **Code / label** of up to 6 characters, unique, required when the schedule shows short codes (for example PTO)
- a full name of up to 50 characters
- a color, which the web app draws as diagonal stripes so an absence stands apart from shifts (the mobile app shows it solid)

## Removing one

Removing a shift, job, or absence that's in use archives it: its history stays, but it can't be used for new scheduling. Shifts that already have an archived job keep it: they still show it, count its hours, and can be edited, swapped, or picked up, but it can't be pasted or dragged onto another day. One that nothing refers to is deleted.

## Who can do what

People with permission to view shifts and jobs can see these sections. Changing them, including allowing shifts without a job, needs permission to manage shifts and jobs. Super Admins can do both.
