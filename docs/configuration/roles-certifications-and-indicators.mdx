---
title: "Roles, Certifications, and Schedule Notes"
description: "Describe your staff with roles and certifications, decide which ones limit who can work a job, and set up schedule notes."
---

Three sections in **Settings** describe your people and your schedule. Your organization may call roles and certifications something else; see [Labels](/configuration/terminology).

## Roles

Roles are tags on a person, such as Charge Nurse or Supervisor. Only some of them affect scheduling.

Select **Edit** to add, rename, reorder, or remove roles. For each role:

| Column                      | What it does                                                                                                                  |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Name**                    | Up to 80 characters, unique.                                                                                                  |
| **Use for job eligibility** | On by default. Only roles with this on can limit who works a job.                                                             |
| **Department**              | **Org-wide**, or the departments it applies to. Shown when you have departments.                                              |
| **Requires**                | The certifications a person needs to hold this role. **Anyone** means none; if you choose several, any one of them is enough. |

**Customize compact labels** adds an abbreviation of up to 20 characters for places where space is tight, or keeps the automatic one.

## Certifications

A certification is the credential shown next to a person's name on the schedule, such as Registered Nurse. It can also limit which roles and jobs a person can take.

Certifications work like roles: add, rename, reorder, give them compact labels, and remove them.

## Schedule notes

Schedule notes flag readings, conflicts, and other details on a person's shift, such as Readings or Shower. Here you set up the kinds of note your organization uses; people then add them to shifts on the schedule.

Select **+ Add schedule note** and give it:

- a name of up to 50 characters, unique
- a color, which is how the note shows on the schedule

## Removing one

Removing a role, certification, or schedule note that's in use asks to **Archive** it; for roles you can **Rename Instead**. Archiving keeps existing uses. An archived schedule note stays on the shifts that have it but can't be added to new ones. Anything unused is deleted instead.

## Who can do what

| Section                  | View                                   | Change                                   |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Roles and certifications | Permission to view organization labels | Permission to manage organization labels |
| Schedule notes           | Permission to view schedule note types | Permission to manage schedule note types |

Super Admins can do everything.
