title: "Coverage"
description: "Set how many people each shift and job needs, and see where the schedule falls short."
---

**Settings > Coverage** sets the minimum number of people you need, by focus area and by each shift and job that can be scheduled there. DubGrid then compares the schedule against it.

## Setting requirements

The section lists every focus area with its shift and job options, grouped by shift, plus a **General jobs** group. For each option, either:

- turn on **Same every day** and enter one **Minimum Staff** number, or
- enter a number for each day from Sunday to Saturday.

Numbers go from 0 to 999. **0** means **No requirement**, so to remove a requirement, set it to 0 and save. Changing requirements doesn't change any shifts on the schedule.

How much a mentored person counts toward coverage is set in [Schedule Rules](/configuration/schedule-rules-and-display).

## Where coverage shows

- **On the schedule**: each section's count rows show how many people are scheduled against how many are needed, and the **Coverage** button shows the number of gaps. **All coverage requirements met** means there are none.
- **On the Dashboard**: coverage and open gaps for the period, and a **Coverage by** focus area card. See [Dashboard](/features/dashboard).

## Who can do what

People with permission to view coverage requirements can see this section; changing it needs permission to manage coverage requirements. Super Admins can do both.
