---
title: "Coverage Requirements"
description: "Set minimum staffing levels per focus area, shift code, and day of week to track whether your schedule meets required coverage."
---

Coverage requirements let you define the minimum number of staff you need for a given focus area, shift code, and day of the week. Once configured, DubGrid automatically compares actual staffing against your requirements and surfaces the results in two places: the **Coverage panel** on the schedule grid and the **Coverage** card on the dashboard.

Find these settings at **Settings → Coverage**.

Requires the `canManageCoverageRequirements` permission.

---

## How coverage is surfaced

**Coverage panel on the schedule grid**
The coverage panel (accessible from the schedule view) shows actual vs. required staff counts for each focus area and shift code combination. Met requirements are shown in green; unmet requirements are flagged in red, so you can spot gaps at a glance without scanning individual rows.

**Dashboard coverage card**
The dashboard includes a donut chart showing coverage status broken down by focus area. Each segment reflects whether requirements are currently met or unmet across the active schedule period.

## Adding a coverage requirement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Coverage settings">
    Go to **Settings → Coverage** and click **Add Requirement**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a focus area">Choose the wing or section this requirement applies to.</Step>
  <Step title="Select a shift code">
    Choose the shift code you want to set a minimum for (e.g., `D` for Day Shift, `N` for Night
    Shift).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a day of the week">
    Select the day this requirement applies to. You can add separate requirements for the same focus
    area and shift code on different days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the minimum staff count">
    Enter the minimum number of staff required to meet coverage for this combination.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The requirement immediately becomes active in the coverage panel and dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Setting requirements by day of the week lets you handle different weekend and weekday staffing
  needs. For example, you might require three Day Shift nurses Monday through Friday, but only two
  on Saturday and Sunday.
</Tip>

---

## Editing and removing requirements

Click any existing requirement in **Settings → Coverage** to edit its minimum count, shift code, or day of week. To remove a requirement, open it and click **Delete**.

Deleting a requirement removes it from the coverage panel immediately — no published or draft shifts are affected.
