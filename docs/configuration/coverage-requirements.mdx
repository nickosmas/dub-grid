---
title: "Coverage"
description: "Set how many people each shift and job needs, and see where the schedule falls short."
---

**Settings > Coverage** sets how many people you need, by focus area and by each shift and job that can be scheduled there, and optionally the most [Auto-build](/features/coverage-and-open-shifts#auto-build) schedules. DubGrid then compares the schedule against it.

## Setting requirements

The section lists every focus area with its shift and job options, grouped by shift, plus a **General jobs** group. For each option, either:

- turn on **Same every day** and enter one **Minimum Staff** number, or
- enter a number for each day from Sunday to Saturday.

Numbers go from 0 to 999. **0** means **No requirement**, so to remove a requirement, set it to 0 and save. Changing requirements doesn't change any shifts on the schedule.

### Maximum

Once a requirement needs someone, you can turn on **Set a maximum**, for example to keep a shift to one supervisor. **Maximum Staff** starts at the minimum and can't go below it: raise the minimum past it and the maximum rises too. With different numbers by day, it applies on every day. The row then shows **Max** after its numbers, and the Schedule Builder shows it after what the requirement needs.

Auto-build and its suggestions never schedule more people than the maximum. You can still add more by hand. Turn **Set a maximum** off for no limit.

How much a mentored person counts toward coverage is set in [Schedule Rules](/configuration/schedule-rules-and-display).

## Where coverage shows

- **On the schedule**: each section's count rows show how many people are scheduled against how many are needed. the **Schedule Builder** shows every requirement against every day, with the number of short shifts on its button, and builds the draft. See [Schedule Builder](/features/coverage-and-open-shifts#schedule-builder).
- **On the Dashboard**: coverage and open gaps for the period, and a **Coverage by** focus area card. See [Dashboard](/features/dashboard).

People who edit shifts can review draft coverage. Staff see published coverage only. See [Coverage and Open Shifts](/features/coverage-and-open-shifts) for how coverage differs from a shift someone can claim.

## Who can do what

People with permission to view coverage requirements can see this section; changing it needs permission to manage coverage requirements. Super Admins can do both.
