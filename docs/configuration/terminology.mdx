---
title: "Labels"
description: "Rename what your organization calls focus areas, certifications, and roles."
---

Every organization describes its staff in its own words. **Settings > Labels** renames three of DubGrid's terms. Labels change wording only: they don't change how anything works.

| Term           | Default            | Could become, for example   |
| -------------- | ------------------ | --------------------------- |
| Focus areas    | **Focus Areas**    | Wings, Units, Departments   |
| Certifications | **Certifications** | Designations                |
| Roles          | **Roles**          | Responsibilities, Positions |

Leave a label blank to go back to its default. Labels can't contain links.

## Where your labels appear

Your labels replace the defaults in the product, for example:

- the **Settings** sections for roles and certifications, and the buttons for adding focus areas under **Departments**
- the eligibility settings on jobs
- the Dashboard's coverage card, "Coverage by" your focus-area label

These guides use the default names throughout.

## Who can change labels

Super Admins, and Admins with permission to manage organization labels. Admins with permission only to view labels see them read-only.
