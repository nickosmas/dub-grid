---
title: "Custom Terminology"
description: "Rename default labels like Focus Areas, Certifications, and Roles to match the language your facility already uses."
---

DubGrid lets you replace three default labels with terms that match your facility's existing vocabulary. For example, if your staff call their sections "Wings" instead of "Focus Areas", you can make that change once and it updates everywhere — in the schedule grid, staff roster, settings pages, and any other place the label appears.

These settings are in **Settings → Organization**.

Requires the `canManageOrgLabels` permission.

<Note>
  Changing a label updates it everywhere in the app immediately. All users in your organization will
  see the new term as soon as you save.
</Note>

---

## Customizable labels

| Default term       | What it refers to                                                                       | Example custom values                      |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| **Focus Areas**    | The wings, departments, or sections that divide your schedule grid                      | Wings, Units, Departments, Floors          |
| **Certifications** | Staff skill levels or qualifications displayed in the staff roster and grid name column | Designations, Credentials, Classifications |
| **Roles**          | Display roles or position titles assigned to staff members                              | Position, Title, Job Title                 |

---

## Where custom labels appear

Once updated, your custom labels replace the defaults throughout the app, including:

- The **Staff roster** column headers and filter options
- The **Schedule grid** name column (certifications and roles are shown alongside each employee's name)
- **Settings** section headings and form labels
- The **Staff detail page** overview tab
- Any in-app help text that references these concepts

---

## Updating a label

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Organization settings">
    Go to **Settings → Organization** and scroll to the **Custom Terminology** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the label">
    Click the field next to the term you want to rename and enter your preferred label.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The new label takes effect immediately for all users in your organization.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you're not sure what term to use, check what your staff already call these things in
  conversation or in your existing spreadsheets — using familiar language reduces confusion when
  onboarding the team.
</Tip>
