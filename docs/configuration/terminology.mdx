---
title: "Labels"
description: "Rename what your organization calls areas, certifications, and roles."
---

Every organization describes its staff in its own words. **Settings > Labels** renames three of DubGrid's terms. Labels change wording only: they don't change how anything works.

| Term           | Default            | Could become, for example                                                   |
| -------------- | ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Areas          | **Areas**          | Units, Wings, Halls, Floors, Neighborhoods, Locations                       |
| Certifications | **Certifications** | Credentials, Licenses, Qualifications, Designations, Skill Levels, Training |
| Roles          | **Roles**          | Positions, Responsibilities, Duties, Titles                                 |

Each field lists examples under it, and takes any name you type. Leave a label blank to go back to its default. Labels can't contain links.

## Where your labels appear

Your labels replace the defaults in the product, for example:

- the **Settings** sections for roles and certifications, and the buttons for adding areas under **Departments**
- the eligibility settings on jobs
- the Dashboard's coverage card, "Coverage by" your area label

These guides use the default names throughout.

## Who can change labels

Super Admins, and Admins with permission to manage organization labels. Admins with permission only to view labels see them read-only.
