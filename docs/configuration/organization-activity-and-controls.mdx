Both of these are for Super Admins only.

## Activity Log

**Settings > Activity Log** is a record of changes across your organization: roles and memberships, billing, and configuration.

- Look at a day, a week, or a month at a time; a week is the default. Use the arrows to move between periods.
- **Search activity** to find entries by what they mention.
- **Filter by activity type** to narrow the list.
- Select an entry to see its details. A change to someone's shifts names the person and shows the day before and after the edit, with each shift's job, times, hours and focus area; a shift on a day that had nothing shows as new. Edits saved before this was added show the new shift only.
- Clearing someone's shifts when they come off a focus area is its own entry, with how many shifts were cleared and in which focus areas.

The log shows up to 200 entries per page, with page controls below. A person's own history also appears on their [person page](/staff/detail-page) under **Activity**.

## Delete Organization

**Settings > Delete Organization** permanently closes your organization. It cancels your subscription and removes access for every member.

1. Select **Delete this organization**.
2. Type `DELETE` followed by your organization's name exactly as shown, to confirm.
3. Confirm your identity when asked.

When it's done, you're signed out on every device. The deletion can't be undone from inside DubGrid; contact DubGrid support if you need the organization restored.
