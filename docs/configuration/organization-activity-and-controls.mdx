Both of these are for Super Admins only.

## Activity Log

**Settings > Activity Log** is a record of changes across your organization: roles and memberships, billing, and configuration.

- Look at a day, a week, or a month at a time; a week is the default. Use the arrows to move between periods.
- **Search activity** to find entries by what they mention.
- **Filter by activity type** to narrow the list.
- Select an entry to see its details. A change to someone's shifts names the person and shows the day before and after the edit, with each shift's job, schedule area, times and hours; a shift on a day that had nothing shows as new. Edits saved before this was added show the new shift only. A [correction to a past day](/features/schedule-grid) reads **Corrected a past day**, with the day before and after and the reason, when one was typed.
- Clearing someone's shifts when they come off a schedule area is its own entry, with how many shifts were cleared and in which schedule areas.
- Three or more changes of the same kind in a row by one person, a few minutes apart, show as one entry, such as an Auto-build reading **Edited the draft** with how many shifts were added, changed and removed, or several people marked away at once. Open it to see each person and what changed for them. A correction or a publish always stands on its own.

The log shows up to 200 entries per page, counting a group as one, with page controls below. A person's own history also appears on their [person page](/staff/detail-page) under **Activity**.

## Delete Organization

**Settings > Delete Organization** permanently closes your organization. It cancels your subscription and removes access for every member.

1. Select **Delete this organization**.
2. Select **Download your data** to keep a copy first, and confirm your identity when asked. It's a ZIP of four spreadsheets: your staff list (in the format **Import staff** reads), every published day, every shift request, and your activity log.
3. Type `DELETE` followed by your organization's name exactly as shown, to confirm.
4. Confirm your identity when asked.

When it's done, you're signed out on every device. For 90 days we can restore the organization, or send you the same download; contact DubGrid support. After 90 days it's deleted for good: its schedule, staff records, requests and activity log, and the sign-in of anyone who belongs to no other organization.
