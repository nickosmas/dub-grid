---
title: "Quick Start"
description: "Set up your DubGrid organization and publish your first schedule as a Super Admin."
---

This guide walks a new Super Admin through getting their organization ready from scratch, from accepting the initial invitation to publishing a first schedule.

<Note>
  Invitation links expire after **72 hours**. If your link has expired, contact DubGrid support to
  request a new one.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your invitation email">
    Look for an email from DubGrid with the subject line containing your organization's name. Click the **Accept Invitation** link inside.

    The link takes you to `dubgrid.com/accept-invite` with a secure token embedded in the URL.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your password">
    On the **Accept Invitation** page:

    1. Your email address is pre-filled and locked.
    2. Enter a password in the **Password** field. Passwords must be **at least 10 characters**. A strength meter shows four levels. Aim for **Strong**.
    3. Re-enter your password in **Confirm password**.
    4. Check the box to agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
    5. Click **Set Password & Accept**.

    After a few seconds, you'll see a confirmation and be redirected to your organization's login page automatically.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from DubGrid. Click the link inside to confirm your address.

    If the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, return to the login page, where you'll be prompted to resend it (one resend allowed every 60 seconds).

  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign in to your organization">
    Navigate to your organization's subdomain, for example, `acme.dubgrid.com` - and sign in with your email and password.

    If you're starting from `dubgrid.com/login`, enter your subdomain in the domain selector first, then continue to the sign-in form.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete the setup wizard">
    After signing in, you'll land on the **Setup** page. It shows a checklist of items that must be completed before the full app unlocks. Work through each item in order:

    **Organization details** - Confirm your organization name and timezone are correct in **Settings**.

    **Focus areas** - Add the wings, departments, or sections that organize your schedule (e.g., *North Wing*, *Memory Care*). Go to **Settings → Staff Config**.

    **Shift codes** - Review the default shift codes (D, E, N, X, and more) and customize colors, times, or add your own codes. Go to **Settings → Shift Codes**.

    **Certifications** - Define the certification levels your staff carry (e.g., *JLCSN*, *CSN II*). Go to **Settings → Staff Config**.

    **Roles** - Define the display roles used in your organization (e.g., *Supervisor*, *Charge Nurse*). Go to **Settings → Staff Config**.

    **Employees** - Add your staff roster. Go to **Staff** and use **Add Employee** to create records individually, or bulk-import a list.

    Each checklist item links directly to the relevant settings page. Return to `/setup` at any time to check your progress. When all items are complete, DubGrid redirects you to the dashboard automatically.

    <Tip>
      You don't have to complete every item in a single session. The setup page saves your progress and you can return to it any time the checklist is incomplete.
    </Tip>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Navigate to the schedule">
    Once setup is complete, go to `/schedule` (or click **Schedule** in the left sidebar). You'll see the schedule grid with your configured focus areas and staff roster.

    The default view is two weeks. Use the **1W**, **2W**, and **Month** buttons in the toolbar to switch views, and the arrow buttons to navigate dates.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Build and publish your first schedule">
    Click any cell in the grid to open the shift edit panel. Select a shift code to assign it. The cell shows the code immediately in **draft** state, indicated visually until published.

    When you're ready to publish:

    1. The **Draft** banner at the top of the schedule shows how many changes are pending.
    2. Click **Publish** in the banner.
    3. Confirm the publish in the dialog that appears.

    Published shifts are now visible to all users in your organization.

    <Warning>
      Clicking **Discard** in the draft banner rolls back all unpublished changes. This cannot be undone.
    </Warning>

  </Step>
</Steps>
