---
title: "Quick Start"
description: "Set up your DubGrid organization as its first Super Admin, from the invitation email to a published schedule."
---

This guide is for the first Super Admin of a new organization. The DubGrid team has created your organization and sent you an invitation.

<Note>
  An invitation link works for **72 hours** from when it was sent, and it doesn't extend. If DubGrid
  sends you a new invitation, only the link in the newest email works.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invitation email">
    The email's subject reads "You're invited to join *your organization* on DubGrid", and it says exactly when the link expires. Select **Accept invitation**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set your password">
    The **Accept invitation** page shows your email address, which you can't change. Choose a password of at least 10 characters that includes a letter and a number, plus an uppercase letter or a symbol. The meter reads **Too short**, **Weak**, **Fair**, or **Strong**; any password it accepts will do.

    Enter the password again in **Confirm password**, agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and select **Set Password & Accept**.

    - If you already have a DubGrid account with this email, the page asks for your existing **DubGrid password** instead and the button reads **Sign In & Accept**.
    - If your account uses an authenticator app, enter its 6-digit code when asked.
    - If the page says **Invitation no longer valid**, the link has expired or been replaced. Ask for a new invitation.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign in">
    When the page says **You're all set**, it takes you to your organization's sign-in page after a few seconds, or select **Sign In Now**. Sign in with your email and password.

    Your organization's address looks like `yourorg.dubgrid.com`. On the main DubGrid sign-in page, enter the subdomain (the `yourorg` part) and select **Continue** first.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Work through the setup guide">
    The first time you sign in, a setup guide opens over the app. As the organization's first Super Admin you complete it before using the rest of DubGrid:

    1. **Welcome**
    2. **Identity**: your organization's name, location, and time zone, and the labels your organization uses for its focus areas, certifications, and roles.
    3. **Structure**: your departments and focus areas, roles, and certifications.
    4. **Schedule**: how shifts are displayed, and your shifts and jobs. Add at least one shift and one job to continue.
    5. **Invite Team**: invite the people who will help you schedule, or skip this for now.
    6. **Done**: select **Go to People** to add your staff.

    DubGrid has no built-in shifts or jobs: you add your organization's own.

  </Step>

<Step title="Start your trial">
  Your organization's 14-day free trial starts the first time a Super Admin signs in. After setup, a
  **Your trial has started!** message shows how many days are left. Select **Set up billing** now,
  or **Maybe later**. See [Subscription](/reference/subscription) for what happens when the trial
  ends.
</Step>

<Step title="Add your people">
  On **People**, add each person on your schedule and invite the ones who should sign in. Someone on
  the schedule doesn't need an account; they appear on the schedule either way. See [Staff
  roster](/staff/roster).
</Step>

  <Step title="Build and publish your first schedule">
    Open **Schedule**. Choose **1 Week**, **2 Weeks**, or **Month** in the toolbar, and select a cell to give that person a shift. Every change is a draft: staff don't see it yet.

    When the schedule is ready, select **Publish** in the draft banner and confirm **Publish Schedule?**. To throw drafts away instead, select **Discard**; you can discard your own drafts and keep everyone else's. See [Draft and publish](/features/draft-publish).

  </Step>
</Steps>
