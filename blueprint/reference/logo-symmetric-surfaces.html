Symmetric stack on every surface

The symmetric mark (a full-size center card with smaller cards in front and behind) on every surface that shows the DubGrid logo, at the sizes those surfaces use. Set the bar to the preset you picked and every surface redraws. Switch to Shipped to compare with today's mark. The mark is unchanged. Only the wordmark moves: its visual center (measured from the rendered letters) lines up with the visible center of the strongest card, which the eye reads as the mark. Word height nudges it from there.

Web app

Favicon, the in-app header (symbol 40 px with the 24 px lockup; symbol alone 36 px on phones), sign-in pages (stacked lockup), and the loading screens.

Landing page and link previews

The marketing nav uses the lockup; the footer uses the symbol in the label gray, so it takes the one-color version. Shared links show the social image.

Email and print

Emails use a PNG of the lockup on white. The printed schedule and PDF report print in one color.

Mobile app

App icons are white on brand blue. Android also crops the icon to the launcher's shape and tints the themed icon to the wallpaper. The splash shows the symbol over the wordmark.
