Stacked logo surfaces

Candidate B (two slanted cards, brand blue behind light blue) placed on every surface that shows the DubGrid logo today, at the sizes those surfaces use. Light and dark where the surface has both.

Web app

Favicon, the in-app header (symbol 40 px with the 24 px lockup; symbol alone 36 px on phones), sign-in pages (stacked lockup), and the loading screens.

Landing page and link previews

The marketing nav uses the lockup; the footer uses the symbol in the label gray, so it takes the one-color version. Shared links show the social image.

Email and print

Emails use a PNG of the lockup on white. The printed schedule and PDF report print in one color.

Mobile app

App icons are white on brand blue. Android also crops the icon to the launcher's shape and tints the themed icon to the wallpaper. The splash shows the symbol over the wordmark.

Before adopting it

The light blue front card is soft on white (about 1.8 : 1). It holds up at header and sign-in sizes, but the 16 px favicon and the light splash lean on the brand blue card. If that reads too faint, deepen the front card on light surfaces only, for example to #7FA6F8, and keep #BCD2FF on dark.

One-color surfaces (footer, print, Android themed icon) cut a gap around the front card so the two cards stay apart without the shading.
