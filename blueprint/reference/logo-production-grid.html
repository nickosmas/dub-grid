Production logo grid

The logo that ships today, drawn from the production geometry in @dubgrid/design-tokens, with a grid of shift cells on its solid front card. Nothing else changes: the outlined back card, the gap, the colors and the lockup spacing are the shipped ones.

Recognition risk. When this logo was chosen, every detail tried inside the front card (a shift bar, a note dot, two lines) made Google Lens match it to another brand's wallet, chat or app icon. A grid was not among them; check the one you pick before adopting it.
