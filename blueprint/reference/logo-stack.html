Stacked card logo

A new direction for the DubGrid symbol: flat, slanted cards that step up and to the right, shading from deep blue at the back to light blue at the front. Pick a candidate or drag the sliders. Each proof shows the full-color mark, the small sizes, the app icon, the one-color version used for print and favicons, and the lockup with the wordmark.

Token values


      

        One color: print, one-color favicons and monochrome app icons can't show the shading, so
        that version cuts a gap around each card instead. With the gap at 0 it uses 0.05 so the
        cards stay apart.
      

      

        Recognition risk: stacked, slanted cards are a common "layers" and "stack" icon, and
        the reference image may itself be another company's mark. Change the card count, slant,
        steps and colors until it isn't a copy, then Lens-check it on its own and in the lockup
        before adopting it.
