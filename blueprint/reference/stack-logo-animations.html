Stacked logo animations

Breathe, Shuffle and Climb for the refined two-card mark. Climb replaces Trace for sync and long waits. The rules carry over: the logo stays static wherever it identifies the product, these play only on full-screen waits, and reduced motion shows the static mark.

Breathe

2.4 s loop

Mobile splash and the web sign-in handoff while the session restores. A slow sine pulse: the front card grows 4.5% while the back card eases away from it along the stack. Every loop starts on the static frame, so the native launch image hands off without a jump.

Shuffle

1.8 s cycle: 1.0 s move, 0.8 s rest

Schedule and route-level loading. The cards trade places along one arc (the front card over the top to the back, the back card underneath to the front), with quartic ease-in-out, then rest. Their colors cross between 30% and 70% of the move, and the gap closes as they pass, so they read as one card shuffling through rather than a snap. When loading ends they finish the current swap and rest on the static mark.

Climb

1.6 s cycle: 1.0 s step, 0.6 s rest

Sync and long-running waits; replaces Trace. The stack steps up the slant as a ripple, front to back. The front card leaves first, stepping forward and shrinking into its far corner; the back card rises into its place and turns light; a new back card arrives last, doing the exact reverse of the leaving card. The step is symmetric under a half turn, like the mark. When syncing ends it finishes the step and rests.
