Stacked logo animations

Breathe and Shuffle rebuilt for the refined two-card mark, and three candidates to replace Trace for sync and long waits. The rules carry over: the logo stays static wherever it identifies the product, these play only on full-screen waits, and reduced motion shows the static mark.

Breathe

2.4 s loop

Mobile splash and the web sign-in handoff while the session restores. A slow sine pulse: the front card grows 4.5% while the back card eases away from it along the stack. Every loop starts on the static frame, so the native launch image hands off without a jump.

Shuffle

1.8 s cycle: 1.0 s move, 0.8 s rest

Schedule and route-level loading. The cards trade places along one arc (the front card over the top to the back, the back card underneath to the front), with quartic ease-in-out, then rest. Their colors cross between 30% and 70% of the move, and the gap closes as they pass, so they read as one card shuffling through rather than a snap. When loading ends they finish the current swap and rest on the static mark.

Sync and long waits: pick one

Replaces Trace

Three replacements for Trace, each different enough from Breathe and Shuffle to tell the states apart. Dropping the third animation is also an option: sync would then use Shuffle.

Climb

1.6 s cycle: 1.0 s move, 0.6 s rest

The stack steps up the slant: the back card rises into the front's place and turns light, the front card shrinks away into its far corner, and a new back card grows in below. It reads as work moving forward, and echoes the cascade in the reference image.
