Stacked card ideas

Ten ways to give the stacked cards a meaning only DubGrid has. Every idea keeps the refined mark (two equal cards, 32° slant, brand blue behind, light blue in front) and adds one thing to it.

What each idea has to do

  • Keep the two slanted cards recognizable, so the work so far carries over.
  • Add one idea from the product: shifts, handoffs, care, weeks, people, or the name.
  • Survive 16 px and one color, where small details disappear first.

My picks to develop

  • Care home: the cards lean together into a roof. Says care facility at a glance, and the silhouette is new.
  • Handoff: the overlap becomes its own color, the shift handover. Costs nothing at small sizes.
  • Shift card: a punched hole turns the card into a time card. A small detail with a lot of meaning.
