Logo concepts

Ten directions for a mark that could only belong to DubGrid. Each comes from one idea in the product's world rather than from a shape. All use the brand blues, so the comparison is about meaning and form, not color.

Why the stacked cards read as generic

  • Two offset cards is the stock "layers" or "copy" icon, in every design tool.
  • Nothing in it points to care, shifts, people or time.
  • The meaning lived in the explanation ("dub" means two), not in the drawing.

My picks to develop

  • Handoff: keeps the two cards, and the overlap becomes the point: the shift handover.
  • Open shift: the product's core job, drawn literally, yet still simple.
  • d|b: built from the name, and as symmetric as the wordmark.
