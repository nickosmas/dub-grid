Symmetric stack motion

Breathe, Shuffle and Climb, adapted to the three-card stack (center card full size, front and back cards 25% smaller, center strongest). Each one starts and ends on the static mark, and Stop lets the current move finish before it rests, as the shipped versions do.

Breathe

Mobile splash and the web sign-in handoff. 2.4 s sine loop: the center card grows 4.5% while the front and back cards ease 4 units away from it along the stack, one each way.

Shuffle

Schedule and route-level loading. Per 1.8 s cycle, a 1.0 s move then 0.8 s rest: the front card arcs over the top to the back, while the center card steps forward to the front and the back card steps up to the center. Each card takes the size and color of the place it moves into (colors cross between 30% and 70% of the move), and the gaps close while the traveling card passes over the stack.

Climb

Sync and long waits. Per 1.6 s cycle, a 1.0 s step then 0.6 s rest. The step ripples up the stack from front to back: the front card steps on and shrinks into its top-right corner, the center card moves up to the front, the back card rises to the center, and a new back card grows out of its bottom-left corner. Leaving and arriving cards mirror each other, and each fades only below 30% of its size, so no frame shows a speck.
